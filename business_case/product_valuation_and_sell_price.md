# Product Valuation and Recommended Sell Price

## Summary

Recommended sell price: **EUR 1.95 million** for a non-exclusive enterprise package that includes the Atoms vs Ashes product, buyer-specific configuration, deployment support, initial portfolio calibration, and 12 months of implementation support.

Recommended negotiation range:

| Package | Recommended price | Use this when |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| 90-day paid pilot | EUR 250k | Buyer wants proof on a bounded site portfolio before enterprise procurement. |
| Enterprise license and deployment | EUR 1.95m | Buyer wants operational internal use, configuration, and first-year support. |
| Full source-code/IP transfer or exclusive national rights | EUR 3.25m+ | Buyer wants control beyond normal enterprise use or wants to restrict resale. |

The EUR 1.95m recommendation is intentionally below the value of a major avoided siting mistake, but high enough to reflect a specialized nuclear decision-support product rather than a generic analytics dashboard.

## Valuation Basis

This valuation is an assumption-based commercial estimate, not a formal appraisal. It uses three sanity checks: replacement cost, buyer value, and income potential.

### 1. Replacement-Cost Check

A buyer could attempt to rebuild a similar product internally or through consultants. A credible rebuild would need software engineering, nuclear siting criteria work, scoring methodology, data-quality handling, reporting, auditability, GUI workflow, test coverage, and technical writing.

Estimated replacement cost:

| Component | Assumed effort/value | Implied cost |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Core product engineering and GUI | 1,200-1,800 senior hours | EUR 180k-360k |
| Criteria/rubric design and QA | 800-1,400 specialist hours | EUR 160k-420k |
| Data integration, testing, audit trail, documentation | 700-1,200 senior hours | EUR 140k-300k |
| Program management, iteration, acceptance workshops | 250-500 hours | EUR 50k-150k |
| Rebuild risk and delay premium | 25%-40% uplift | EUR 130k-490k |

Replacement-cost indication: **EUR 660k-EUR 1.72m**.

That range supports a sell price above EUR 1m because the buyer is not just purchasing code. The buyer is avoiding rebuild delay, methodology risk, and the cost of aligning internal and external experts from scratch.

### 2. Buyer-Value Check

For a nuclear utility, early site-screening value comes from better capital allocation and faster rejection of weak sites. The relevant comparison is not the cost of a software subscription. The comparison is the cost of prematurely advancing the wrong site, over-scoping consultant work, or spending months on a portfolio process that does not produce an auditable decision record.

Conservative buyer-value assumptions:

- Avoided or reduced consultant effort across a multi-site portfolio: **EUR 400k-EUR 900k**.
- Value of shortening an early siting decision cycle by 2-4 months: **EUR 300k-EUR 1.2m**, depending on program urgency and internal cost of delay.
- Value of avoiding one poorly prioritized deep-dive study: **EUR 250k-EUR 750k**.
- Governance value from a defensible, reproducible scoring record: difficult to price, but material in a nuclear program.

Buyer-value indication for the first enterprise deployment: **EUR 1.0m-EUR 2.85m**.

This supports a quoted enterprise price near EUR 2m, provided the sale is positioned around portfolio decision quality and not merely around software features.

### 3. Income-Potential Check

If sold as a specialized enterprise product, the likely commercial model is a high-touch license plus services motion. The product would not behave like low-price SaaS. It would be sold to utilities, SMR developers, government-backed energy transition programs, or engineering consultancies working on siting portfolios.

Assumed unit economics:

| Revenue item | Base assumption |
| --- | ---: |
| Enterprise deployment fee | EUR 1.5m-EUR 2.2m |
| Annual maintenance/support after year 1 | EUR 250k-EUR 400k |
| Optional portfolio refresh or new-country configuration | EUR 300k-EUR 750k |
| Gross margin after configuration effort | 55%-70% |

For one buyer, the three-year account value could plausibly be **EUR 2.4m-EUR 3.8m** if the first deployment leads to continued support and scenario refreshes. Discounting execution risk, procurement friction, and buyer-specific customization, the current product asset value is best viewed as **EUR 1.4m-EUR 2.6m** on a non-exclusive basis.

## Recommended Sell Price

Quote **EUR 1.95m** for the enterprise license and deployment package.

Include in the price:

- Non-exclusive internal enterprise use by the buyer.
- Configuration for the buyer's initial candidate-site portfolio.
- One controlled deployment environment.
- Calibration workshops with technical stakeholders.
- Executive business-case outputs, shortlist reporting, and data-gap register.
- 12 months of support, bug fixing, and reasonable methodology refinement.

Exclude from the price:

- Third-party data purchase costs.
- Live API usage fees or proprietary data licenses.
- Formal regulatory licensing submissions.
- Unlimited new-country expansion.
- Exclusive rights or source-code/IP assignment.

## Negotiation Guidance

Open at **EUR 2.25m** if the buyer is strategic, the procurement process is formal, and the package includes senior advisory time. Target close: **EUR 1.95m**. Do not go below **EUR 1.35m** for an enterprise deployment unless scope is reduced to a pilot-plus-license with limited support.

For a cautious buyer, offer a EUR 250k pilot with a conversion credit: if the buyer proceeds to enterprise deployment within six months, credit 50%-100% of the pilot fee against the EUR 1.95m package. This lowers procurement friction without weakening the headline product value.

For exclusivity, source-code assignment, or national resale restrictions, price separately. Recommended ask: **EUR 3.25m minimum**, with a preferred range of **EUR 3.25m-EUR 4.5m** depending on support duration and restrictions on future sales.

## Final Price Recommendation

Sell the product to a Nuclearelectrica-style buyer at **EUR 1.95 million** for a non-exclusive enterprise license and implementation package. Use **EUR 250k** as the paid-pilot entry point and **EUR 3.25m+** only if the buyer wants exclusivity, full source-code ownership, or resale restrictions.
