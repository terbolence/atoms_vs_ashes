# NUWARD (EDF) — Evidence Corpus

**Vendor:** EDF SA (technical lead) with TechnicAtome, Naval Group, Framatome, CEA — branded NUWARD as an EDF subsidiary programme.
**Reference plant:** **2 × 200 MWe = 400 MWe** integrated PWR (revised, post-July-2024 design reset; pre-reset reference was 2 × 170 MWe).
**Coolant / spectrum:** Light water (PWR) / thermal
**Fuel:** UO₂ ceramic, U-235 enrichment < 5% (Framatome supply)
**Lead first-of-a-kind project:** **EDF site in France** — Marcoule first NUWARD plant indicated for early 2030s; **revised target slipped to mid-2030s** following 2024 design reset.
**Joint Early Review:** EU/EU-coordinated early regulatory engagement with **ASN/ASNR (FR)**, **STUK (FI)**, **ÚJD (CZ)** — coordinated by IAEA SMR Regulators' Forum.

## 1. NEA SMR Dashboard 3rd Edition extract

> **Reactor description:** Land-based pressurised light water reactor.
> Thermal power: 540 MWth (per module) — Core outlet temperature: 307 °C — Spectrum: thermal — Fuel form: Uranium-oxide ceramic — Base fissile material: U-235, enrichment < 5%.

(NEA Dashboard 3rd Ed (2025), p. 192. *Note:* the NEA Dashboard reflects the **pre-reset** NUWARD design parameters; the post-July-2024 reset has changed the reactor sizing and configuration. April 2026 status below.)

> **Licensing.** In 2022, the French Authority for Nuclear Safety (ASN, now Authority for Nuclear and Radiation Protection or ASNR), together with the Czech State Office for Nuclear Safety (SÚJB) and the Finnish Radiation and Nuclear Safety Authority (STUK), launched a Joint Early Review (JER) of the NUWARD design. The JER aims to advance the European harmonisation of safety requirements for SMRs, including reactor design and fuel cycle. In November 2023, NUWARD launched the second phase of JER, focusing on key safety topics, including: passive safety system; spent fuel pool; emergency planning; integral testing for licensing; and ageing of polymers. Phase two is expected to be completed by mid-2025. The Polish National Atomic Energy Agency joined the JER process in February 2024 as observer.

> **Siting.** In December 2022, EDF and Vattenfall signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) on the deployment of NUWARD SMR units at potential sites in Sweden. In 2023, NUWARD signed a Letter of Intent with Italian companies Edison and Ansaldo Nucleare for the deployment of SMRs in Italy. EDF (now NUWARD) is also engaged with the European Industrial Alliance, the European SMR partnership, and the Belgian SMR Steering Committee. In April 2024, EDF, Italian utility company Edison, French nuclear company Framatome, Italian engineering company Ansaldo Energia, and the Italian state-controlled energy infrastructure operator Ansaldo Nucleare signed a Letter of Intent to develop SMR-based nuclear projects in Italy and across Europe. In 2024, NUWARD entered the second selection phase for Sweden's SMR development at the Ringhals nuclear site.

> **Financing.** Approximately EUR 500 million (USD 2023 540 million) in private and public matching funds have been committed since 2017 for NUWARD design's pre-conceptual and conceptual phases. NUWARD has been awarded EUR 50 million (USD 2023 54 million) under the French government's France 2030 plan. In 2024, the French government decided to grant EUR 300 million (USD 2023 327 million) to NUWARD over the period of 2024-2026. NUWARD also benefits from financial contributions from Framatome and TechnicAtome.

> **Supply chain.** Joint development companies and suppliers in the NUWARD project include Framatome, TechnicAtome, Naval Group, Tractebel, Edvance, EDF, and CEA. Some equipment for NUWARD is being supplied by ENDEL, Ponticelli, Egis, Vinci Construction, Bouygues Travaux Public, Eiffage Energie Système, Eiffage Génie Civil, AlsymEx, Thermodyn, Boccard, Cegelec, AMIS, Daher, and Westinghouse.

> **Engagement.** EDF (now NUWARD) is in the process of providing financial support to multiple universities and research centres, including AMIS in Belgium, the Royal Institute of Technology in Sweden, and a multi-stakeholder partnership with multiple educational institutions in France for an Engineering Higher Education Programme. NUWARD also presented its design at trade fairs and events in Czechia, France, Italy, Poland, and Sweden. NUWARD has also signed cooperation agreements with companies in support of its commercial deployment, including with Doosan Enerbility, ČEZ, Edison, EDP, EnBW, Vattenfall, ULC-Energy, IGS Energy, and Industria Group. NUWARD has also engaged with French communes and the public, ahead of formal applications to the ASNR.

> **Fuel.** The NUWARD design uses fuel that is the current industry standard for water-cooled reactor technologies. Given this, there are no barriers expected in the fuel supply chain for this SMR. Pre-application meetings with regulators have not flagged any issues with the fuel cycle.

(NEA Dashboard 3rd Ed (2025), pp. 192-193.)

## 2. April 2026 evidence snapshot

### Joint Early Review Phase 2 closure

- **December 2025:** ASNR (FR), STUK (FI), SÚJB (CZ) **closed Joint Early Review Phase 2** for the (pre-reset) NUWARD design. Closure reports published. ([NUWARD JER Phase 2 closure report](https://www.nuward.com/sites/nuward/files/2025-12/RegulatorsJERphase2.pdf); saved as `NUWARD_JER_Phase2_Closure_Report.pdf` in `../references/`)
- The closure report identifies **convergent regulator views** on the topics covered, plus **divergence on EPZ definition** and on **passive safety system reliability metrics** — these will need to be re-addressed under the post-reset design.

### Design reset (July 2024)

- **July 2024:** EDF **paused the original NUWARD design and ordered a reset** following an internal cost/competitiveness review. The reset moved the design from a bespoke integral PWR with proprietary passive safety architecture to a more **conventional integrated PWR** that re-uses Framatome PWR-fuel components.
- **Reset ramifications:**
  - Original 2 × 170 MWe (340 MWe gross) revised to **2 × 200 MWe (400 MWe gross)**.
  - EDF formally re-engaged ASNR and the JER cohort in late 2025; **JER Phase 3** under preparation as of April 2026.
  - **First COD slipped from 2030 to mid-2030s** (vendor public guidance; no firm public date).

### Italian and Czech engagement

- **April 2024:** Edison, Ansaldo Energia, Ansaldo Nucleare LoI to deploy SMRs in Italy and Europe.
- **2024-2025:** ČEZ, Vattenfall, ULC-Energy, EnBW, EDP cooperation agreements **continued through the design reset**.
- **Vattenfall Ringhals:** NUWARD made it to the Ringhals second selection phase in 2024 — but the **April 2026 status is that Vattenfall's two finalists are RR SMR and BWRX-300**, not NUWARD. The reset removed NUWARD from the active running for Ringhals.

### Funding

- **2024:** French government commitment of **EUR 300 million for 2024-2026**.
- Total committed since 2017: ~EUR 850 million across French government and industrial partners.

## 4. Open data gaps

- **Romania:** **no NUWARD activity in Romania** as of April 2026; Doicești is committed to NuScale.
- **Post-reset design freeze**: the 2 × 200 MWe configuration has not yet undergone JER review at any depth; Phase 3 timing is uncertain.
- **First COD date for any NUWARD plant**: vendor language is "mid-2030s" without a firm date — the design freeze risk is **the largest single risk factor** for NUWARD.
- **CAPEX disclosure:** EDF has not published an overnight CAPEX figure for the reset design. The model uses 8 000 USD/kWe FOAK, low/high 6 500 / 12 000 USD/kWe.
- **Tier A A5 (Design freeze)** for the post-reset design: **fail** as of April 2026 for any 2033 COD plan; **caution** for late-2030s COD.
