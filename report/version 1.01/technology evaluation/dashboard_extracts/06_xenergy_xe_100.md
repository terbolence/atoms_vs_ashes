# X-energy Xe-100 — Evidence Corpus

**Vendor:** X Energy LLC (US, Rockville MD HQ)
**Reference plant:** **4 × 80 MWe = 320 MWe gross** per standard reference plant (4-pack Xe-100 modules)
**Coolant / spectrum:** Helium / thermal — Generation IV high-temperature gas-cooled reactor (HTGR), pebble-bed
**Fuel:** **TRISO-X pebbles**, U-235 enrichment **≥10% to <20% (HALEU)**
**HALEU dependency:** YES — see `00_haleu_supply_snapshot.md` for A2 implications
**Lead first-of-a-kind project:** **Dow Seadrift, Texas** (industrial heat + electricity)
**Major commercial offtake (data centres):** Amazon/AWS Master Agreement (>5 GW by 2039)

## 1. NEA SMR Dashboard 3rd Edition extract

> **Reactor description:** Helium gas-cooled high-temperature pebble bed reactor.
> Thermal power: 200 MWth — Core outlet temperature: 750 °C — Spectrum: thermal — Fuel form: Uranium-oxide ceramic TRISO pebble — Base fissile material: U-235, enrichment ≥ 10% to < 20%.

(NEA Dashboard 3rd Ed (2025), p. 232.)

> **Licensing.** In November 2023, X-energy submitted to the US Nuclear Regulatory Commission (NRC) the first part of an early licensing topical report on the Xe-100 reactor's safety design strategy. In 2023, X-energy completed the second phase of the Canadian Nuclear Safety Commission's (CNSC) pre-licensing vendor design review for the Xe-100. Long Mott Energy is preparing a Construction Permit Application to the NRC for the construction of an Xe-100 plant in Seadrift, Texas, USA. In 2024, X-energy and Energy Northwest commenced pre-application engagement with NRC for the deployment of an Xe-100 plant in central Washington State, USA.

> **Siting.** In 2023, X-energy and Dow Inc.'s subsidiary Long Mott Energy formed a joint development agreement to deploy four Xe-100 reactors at Dow's Seadrift facility in Texas. In 2024, X-energy and Amazon announced a USD 500 million strategic investment to support the development and licensing of Xe-100 plants. The investment will support the deployment of more than 5 gigawatts (GW) of new power projects by 2039 in the United States. The first project will be in central Washington State and includes plans to deliver up to 320 megawatts (MW) of power.

> **Financing.** Energy Northwest, X-energy and Amazon Web Services have signed a development agreement that includes options for further funding to enable construction. The Tennessee Valley Authority is also exploring potential deployment of an Xe-100 plant in the United States. In 2025, X-energy formed a SMR Power Generation Hub with Sealaska, Northwest Innovation Works, and Iroquois Gas Transmission System to explore SMR deployment in the United States. In Korea, the Korea Hydro & Nuclear Power Co., Doosan Enerbility and X-energy formed a working group on Xe-100 deployment. X-energy has signed Memoranda of Understanding for potential deployment with: Strathcona Resources Ltd (Cold Lake) and Capital Power in Canada; Vita Inclinata Industrial in Mexico; OPG in the United States; LX International in Korea; OBL Holdings in the Saudi Arabia; and IGS Energy in the United States.

> **Engagement.** In 2024, X-energy and the Korea Atomic Industrial Forum signed a partnership to advance global SMR deployment. X-energy has been holding "Get to know our Xe-100" events targeted at communities in proximity to potential deployment sites in the United States and Canada. X-energy has presented its activities at multiple congressional events including in Texas, where the Texas Public Policy Foundation has expressed support to deployment in the state.

> **Fuel.** X-energy designed the TRISO-X fuel for use in Xe-100 reactors. TRISO-X is a high-assay low-enriched uranium (HALEU) tri-structural isotropic (TRISO) pebble-shaped fuel. In 2022, the NRC accepted X-energy's licence application to construct and operate a TRISO-X commercial-scale fuel production facility (TF3) in Oak Ridge, Tennessee. In 2024, the US Department of Energy (DOE) announced a USD 148.5 million (USD 2023 144 million) cooperative agreement with X-energy to support the construction of TF3. In 2024, X-energy was also awarded a USD 50.5 million (USD 2023 49 million) cooperative agreement with the DOE Office of Clean Energy Demonstrations to support work on TF3.

(NEA Dashboard 3rd Ed (2025), pp. 232-233.)

## 2. April 2026 evidence snapshot

### TRISO-X TF3 fuel facility (Oak Ridge, TN)

- **27 February 2026:** NRC issued the **Construction Permit for the TRISO-X TF3** commercial fuel facility — the **first new commercial reactor fuel facility licence in over 60 years**. ([X-energy press 2026-02-27](https://x-energy.com/news/x-energy-marks-key-permitting-milestone-for-the-first-of-its-kind-commercial-haleu-fuel-fabrication-facility); [WNN 2026-03](https://world-nuclear-news.org/articles/nrc-issues-construction-permit-for-x-energy-fuel-facility); [WNA Fuel Cycle Facilities](https://world-nuclear.org/information-library/facts-and-figures/fuel-cycle-facilities-around-the-world))
- **Operations target:** **mid-2028**.
- This is the milestone that converts X-energy's HALEU fuel pipeline from R&D-scale to commercial-scale; it is the single largest event in the April 2026 snapshot for any HALEU-dependent design.

### Long Mott Energy / Dow Seadrift (Texas)

- **31 March 2025:** **Long Mott Energy submitted CPA** for an Xe-100 plant at Dow Seadrift to the NRC. ([Long Mott CPA acceptance announcement](https://www.longmottenergy.com/long-mott-cpa-acceptance-announcement)) — first Generation IV CPA accepted by NRC for a commercial advanced reactor.
- **June 2025:** NRC **accepted the application for review**. ([NRC Long Mott project page](https://www.nrc.gov/reactors/new-reactors/advanced/who-were-working-with/applicant-projects/long-mott))
- **NRC review schedule:** Final SER target **2027**; CP issuance target Q1 2028.
- **First electricity / steam target:** ~2030–2031.

### Amazon Web Services (Washington State + portfolio)

- **October 2024:** Amazon announced a **USD 500 million strategic investment** in X-energy. Initial Energy Northwest project planned in central Washington State to deliver up to **320 MW** by early 2030s. Programme totals **>5 GW by 2039**. ([Amazon press 2024-10-16](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/sustainability/amazon-nuclear-small-modular-reactor-net-carbon-zero); [WNA Xe-100](https://world-nuclear.org/information-library/facts-and-figures/reactor-database/x-energy-xe-100))
- **AWS-Energy Northwest pre-application engagement** with NRC began 2024.

### Other 2025-2026 milestones

- **March 2025:** X-energy completed CNSC VDR Phase 2 (Canada).
- **2025:** Established SMR Power Generation Hub (Sealaska, Northwest Innovation Works, Iroquois Gas Transmission).
- **Korea:** KHNP, Doosan Enerbility, X-energy working group on Xe-100 deployment.

## 4. Open data gaps

- **Romania / European deployment:** none publicly documented for Xe-100. The design's HALEU dependency makes a 2033 European COD highly contingent on TRISO-X TF3 reaching nameplate by 2030.
- **CAPEX disclosure:** X-energy has not published Xe-100 CAPEX in detail. The model uses 7 500 USD/kWe FOAK 4-pack, low/high 6 000 / 10 500 USD/kWe.
- **TRISO-X TF3 ramp curve:** mid-2028 first ops to nameplate is 3-5 years; this gates first cores for commercial Xe-100 fleet beyond Long Mott.
- **AWS-Energy Northwest siting and CP timeline:** not yet docketed.
