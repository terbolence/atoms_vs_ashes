# Rolls-Royce SMR (RR SMR) — Evidence Corpus

**Vendor:** Rolls-Royce SMR Ltd (UK; ČEZ acquired 20% equity in 2024)
**Reference plant:** **470 MWe net** (single 1 358 MWth close-coupled three-loop PWR per plant)
**Coolant / spectrum:** Light water (PWR) / thermal
**Fuel:** UO₂ ceramic, U-235 enrichment < 5%, supplier Westinghouse (2023 agreement)
**Lead first-of-a-kind project:** **Wylfa, Anglesey** (UK) — landmark GBE-N contract signed 13 April 2026
**CEE relevance:** **ČEZ Temelín** (Czech Republic), up to 3 GWe deployment programme

## 1. NEA SMR Dashboard 3rd Edition extract

> **Reactor description:** Close-coupled, three-loop pressurised water reactor using low enriched uranium fuel.
> Thermal power: 1 358 MWth — Core outlet temperature: 325 °C — Spectrum: thermal — Fuel form: Uranium-oxide ceramic — Base fissile material: U-235, enrichment < 5%.

(NEA Dashboard 3rd Ed (2025), p. 216.)

> **Licensing.** In July 2024, Rolls-Royce SMR (RR SMR) advanced to Step 3 of the UK Office for Nuclear Regulation (ONR) Generic Design Assessment (GDA) process, two years after submitting its design certification application for review. Regulators from Czechia, Finland, Poland, Sweden, and the Netherlands have observed GDA meetings to familiarise themselves with the RR SMR design and the GDA assessment process. This engagement may facilitate future deployment in their respective countries. Additionally, in 2024, the International Atomic Energy Agency conducted a Technical Safety Review of the Rolls-Royce SMR design.

> **Siting.** In October 2024, Czech operator CEZ Group chose Rolls-Royce SMR for up to 3 GWe at its Temelín site, with early work planned to start in 2025. That same month, Great British Nuclear (GBN) shortlisted Rolls-Royce SMR among four SMR technologies for the next phase of the United Kingdom's deployment competition, with GBN selecting the sites. Also in 2024, Sweden's Vattenfall shortlisted Rolls-Royce SMR as one of two potential SMR suppliers for its Ringhals site. Poland's Ministry of Climate and Environment approved Industria's plans to deploy Rolls-Royce SMRs for its Central Hydrogen Cluster. UK-based Chiltern Vital Group (CVG) partnered with Rolls-Royce SMR for potential deployment at the Gloucestershire Science and Technology Park, which CVG acquired in 2024.

> **Financing.** Rolls-Royce SMR has attracted more than GBP 490 million (USD 2023 713 million) in combined public and private sector funding to support its development and deployment. The UK government has supported the RR SMR, including with GBP 210 million (USD 2023 305 million) in funding through the UK Research and Innovation funding. In the private sector, partners in the Rolls-Royce SMR business have made equity investments. Specifically, Rolls-Royce Group, BNF Resources, Constellation, and the Qatar Investment Authority have raised nearly GBP 280 million (USD 2023 407 million) in combined funding for the RR SMR. In October 2024, ČEZ Group acquired a 20% equity position in Rolls-Royce SMR.

> **Supply chain.** In 2024, Rolls-Royce SMR signed contracts with GSE Solutions for a power station simulator and the University of Sheffield to establish Module Development Facility concepts at the University Advanced Manufacturing Research Centre. The facility hosted the first supplier conference for the RR SMR in June 2024. Rolls-Royce SMR has secured multiple agreements for design and engineering services with: AtkinsRéalis, BAM Nuttall, Laing O'Rourke, Jacobs, Nuclear AMRC, Assystem, The Welding Institute, and the UK National Nuclear Laboratory. It also has Memoranda of Understandings (MoUs) with: Sheffield Forgemasters, and Škoda JS. In 2024, Rolls-Royce SMR was selected to form a Project-Working Group under the European Industrial Alliance on SMRs.

> **Engagement.** Since 2021, Rolls-Royce SMR has received endorsements from UK government officials, including former Business and Energy Secretary Kwasi Kwarteng, and has been working with local stakeholders in six communities to identify potential sites for an SMR module factory. Additionally, Solway Community Power Company, an SMR development facilitator, chose Rolls-Royce SMR for deployment in West Cumbria, United Kingdom. Rolls-Royce SMR has also signed MoUs with: ULC-Energy in the Netherlands, the Jordan Atomic Energy Commission in Jordan, Constellation in the United States, and Energoatom in Ukraine.

> **Fuel.** The RR SMR utilises fuel that is the current industry standard for water-cooled reactor technologies that are similar in design. Given this, there are no barriers expected in the fuel supply chain for this SMR. In 2023, Rolls-Royce SMR signed an agreement with Westinghouse Electric Company to develop and advance work on the design of the RR SMR nuclear fuel.

(NEA Dashboard 3rd Ed (2025), pp. 216-217.)

## 2. April 2026 evidence snapshot

### UK programme

- **GDA progress:** Rolls-Royce SMR is in **Step 3 (detailed assessment)** of the UK GDA, which began **31 July 2024**. Step 2 was completed earlier in 2024. ([RR-SMR GDA process page](https://gda.rolls-royce-smr.com/gda-process))
- **March 2026:** UK government granted **"nuclear justification"** for the RR SMR design — an additional regulatory step under EU/UK retained law. ([NucNet 2026-03-05](https://www.nucnet.org/news/uk-government-grants-nuclear-justification-for-rolls-royce-smr-design-3-5-2026))
- **June 2025:** Rolls-Royce SMR **selected as preferred bidder** by Great British Energy – Nuclear (GBE-N, formerly GBN) following a multi-vendor competition. ([Yahoo Finance 2024](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/rolls-royce-smr-ez-agree-114555019.html))
- **13 April 2026:** Rolls-Royce SMR signed a **two-stage landmark contract with GBE-N** for site-specific design and preparation of the **first units at Wylfa, Anglesey**. The press release confirms RR SMR is "the furthest through any European regulatory process". ([Rolls-Royce press release 2026-04-13](https://www.rolls-royce.com/media/press-releases/2026/13-04-2026-rr-welcomes-contract-with-uk-government-for-delivery-of-small-modular-reactors.aspx))

### Czech Republic (ČEZ)

- **September/October 2024:** ČEZ selected Rolls-Royce SMR as its SMR technology partner. ([WNA Czechia](https://world-nuclear.org/our-association/publications/world-nuclear-performance-report/czechia-world-nuclear-performance-report))
- **October 2024:** ČEZ acquired a **20% equity position** in Rolls-Royce SMR Ltd.
- **July 2025:** **Early Works Agreement signed** for site-specific activities at the **Temelín** nuclear power plant. Goal: up to **3 GWe** of SMR capacity (≈6 RR SMR units) with first unit expected **mid-2030s**. ([WNN 2025-07](https://www.world-nuclear-news.org/articles/cez-and-rolls-royce-smr-sign-temelin-early-works-agreement); [Rolls-Royce SMR press release](https://www.rolls-royce-smr.com/press/rolls-royce-smr-and-cez-sign-early-works-agreement-to-advance-smr-deployment-in-czech-republic))
- **August 2025:** ČEZ partner ecosystem expanded — Škoda JS, ÚJV Řež contracted as Czech industrial partners; Curtiss-Wright contracted as UK partner. ([E&T magazine 2025-08](https://eandt.theiet.org/2025/08/04/rolls-royce-smr-signs-further-deals-czech-and-uk-firms-global-fleet-small-modular))

### Sweden, Netherlands, Poland

- **Vattenfall** has shortlisted RR SMR (alongside BWRX-300) as a finalist for **Ringhals**.
- **ULC-Energy / Netherlands:** MoU active.
- **Industria Polska:** approval to deploy RR SMRs for the Central Hydrogen Cluster (Poland).

### LCOE target

- Rolls-Royce SMR public LCOE target: **< £70/MWh (~83 EUR/MWh)** — the most competitive published vendor target. ([NucNet 2025-03-07](https://www.nucnet.org/news/analysis-shows-competitive-lcoe-target-for-small-modular-reactors-7-3-2025))

## 4. Open data gaps

- **CAPEX disclosure:** RR SMR has not published a detailed overnight CAPEX number; only the < £70/MWh LCOE target. The model uses an inferred 7 500 USD/kWe FOAK for first UK unit (consistent with vendor's LCOE math given UK WACC), with low/high 6 000 / 9 500 USD/kWe.
- **Romanian interest:** none publicly documented; design is being evaluated for CEE through the Czech Temelín route.
- **Westinghouse fuel-supply finalisation:** the 2023 collaboration agreement does not yet name the European fuel-fabrication site for non-UK deployments.
- **First-fuel COD timing for Wylfa:** publicly "early 2030s" per vendor; the model assumes first concrete 2028, COD 2032 for the FOAK.
