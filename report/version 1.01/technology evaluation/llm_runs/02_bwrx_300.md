## Analyst Summary

**Headline finding.** BWRX-300 is the **single highest-scoring SMR design** in the April 2026 evaluation (final score 4.66, `shortlist_primary`). Its strength comes from the unique combination of (a) the only SMR with a CNSC construction licence in hand and active construction at Darlington, (b) a docketed NRC Construction Permit Application at TVA Clinch River, (c) UK GDA Step 2 progressing, (d) Polish DiP for 24 units at 6 sites, and (e) the strongest vendor balance sheet (GE Vernova + Hitachi joint venture) of the nine candidates. **No Tier A gate is at caution.** It is the textbook case of a design ready to deploy.

**Key strengths.** First, **fleet deployability is the strongest of any candidate**: 4 units at Darlington + 24 in Poland + 1 at Clinch River + Vattenfall finalist + Estonian Fermi Energia application — totalling ~30+ units in active planning. This drives the +5 % C4 modifier and supports learning-curve-driven cost reduction. Second, **standard BWR fuel via a fully non-Russian supply chain** (Cameco + Urenco USA + Orano + GNF-A) gives the highest possible energy-sovereignty score. Third, the **vendor consortium credibility** is unmatched — GE Vernova alone has ~USD 60 B market cap, with 60-year commitment to BWR fleet servicing established.

**Key weaknesses.** First, **Romanian deployment intent is not publicly documented**. SNN is contractually committed to NuScale at Doicești; for Romania, the BWRX-300 is a hypothetical alternative for a future second site. Second, **the actual learning curve from Darlington unit 1 (CAD 6.1 B) to unit 4 (CAD 4.1 B) is the only data point**, suggesting ~33 % cost reduction — but this curve has not yet been realised. Third, the **EPZ-at-site-boundary claim** is accepted by CNSC for Darlington but has not been docketed by any EU regulator.

**Most critical data gap.** The **forward Polish 6-site programme schedule** is the single largest item — if Orlen Synthos Green Energy delivers its first Polish unit by 2030-2031 with substantial cost reduction, the BWRX-300 becomes the de-facto European SMR standard and a **strong contender for any Romanian second SMR site**.

**What would change the verdict.** The verdict would degrade to `shortlist_secondary` only if (i) the Darlington unit 1 commissioning slips beyond 2032 or (ii) actual unit 1 cost overshoots CAD 8 B materially. It would degrade to `pending` if EU State-aid clearance for the Polish 24-unit programme stalls or if first-Polish-unit construction is not initiated by 2027.
