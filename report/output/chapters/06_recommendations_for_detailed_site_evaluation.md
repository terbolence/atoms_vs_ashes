# 6. Recommendations for Detailed Site Evaluation

This chapter turns screening findings into next-step work packages for Stage 3 characterization and stakeholder follow-up.

## 6.1 Recommended Stage 3 Investigations

List investigation families: geotechnical, seismic, hydrological, meteorological, environmental, emergency-planning, grid, cooling, land, ownership, and stakeholder work.

## 6.2 Data Gaps Requiring Field Confirmation

Prioritise data gaps that could change exclusion, safety-floor, or ranking outcomes.

## 6.3 Recommended Regulatory and Stakeholder Follow-Up

Identify early regulator engagement, grid/operator engagement, land/ownership checks, local authority consultation, and emergency-planning coordination.

## 6.4 Prioritisation of Next-Step Work

Translate results into a staged action plan: immediate data validation, targeted field work, country/site due diligence, and decision gates.
