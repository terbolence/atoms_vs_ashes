# Override notes - EP-01 Emergency Planning Feasibility

This override extends `04_emergency_planning.md` for the EP-01
composite. Apply in addition to the family base prompt.

## Reading depth

Spend 4-6 sentences. EP-01 is the rolled-up composite: explaining it
well is the difference between an executive understanding the
emergency-planning story and not.

## Required raw values to cite

- EP feasibility composite (/100).
- Road sub-score (/100).
- Special-population sub-score (/100).
- Geography sub-score (/100).
- Population sub-score (/100).
- Evacuation feasible flag (yes / no).

## Plain-English bands (composite)

| Composite (/100) | Read |
| --- | --- |
| > 75 | strong feasibility; standard Stage 3 emergency-plan refresh |
| 50 - 75 | workable feasibility with one or two binding sub-scores |
| < 50 | constrained feasibility; sub-score(s) drive the result |

## Stage 3 implications

- Identify the lowest sub-score in prose; it is what dominates the
  composite.
- Translate the binding sub-score into a Stage 3 work item:
  - Low road sub-score: ETE study + road reinforcement scoping.
  - Low special-population sub-score: hospital / prison / care home
    evacuation planning workstream.
  - Low geography sub-score: alternative-route survey, possible
    barrier mitigation.
  - Low population sub-score: couple to RI-04, MACCS-style
    consequence modelling.

## Do not

- Average the sub-scores in prose; the bundle already reports the
  composite.
- Imply that an EP-01 above 75 closes the criterion; the local
  emergency plan still needs the host country regulator's review.
