# Override notes - EP-02 Evacuation Routes

This override extends `04_emergency_planning.md` for Evacuation
Routes. Apply in addition to the family base prompt.

## Reading depth

Spend 3-5 sentences.

## Required raw values to cite

- Road density in EPZ (km / km2).
- Road length in EPZ (km).
- Motorway access flag (yes / no).

## Plain-English bands

| Road density (km / km2) | Read |
| --- | --- |
| > 1.0 | dense road network; standard ETE study |
| 0.6 - 1.0 | comfortable rural network |
| 0.3 - 0.6 | sparse network; route reinforcement likely |
| < 0.3 | very sparse; potential evacuation bottleneck |

## Stage 3 implications

- Dense / comfortable: Stage 3 commissions a standard ETE study and
  confirms motorway-access redundancy.
- Sparse / very sparse: Stage 3 must scope route reinforcement,
  test contraflow scenarios, and couple to RI-04 to evaluate dose
  burden during constrained evacuation.

## Do not

- Recommend specific road or motorway construction.
- Confuse road density with road quality; the evidence is geometry
  only.
