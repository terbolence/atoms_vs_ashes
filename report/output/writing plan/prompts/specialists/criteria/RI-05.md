# Override notes - RI-05 Distance to Population Centres

This override extends `03_radiological_impact.md` for Distance to
Population Centres. Apply in addition to the family base prompt.

## Reading depth

Spend 3-5 sentences.

## Required raw values to cite

- Nearest city above 50k people (km).
- Nearest city population (people).
- Nearest city name.

## Plain-English bands

| Distance to nearest 50k+ city | Read |
| --- | --- |
| > 30 km | comfortably outside the typical low-population-zone reference |
| 15 - 30 km | within typical IAEA EPZ proximity; supports a workable EPZ |
| 5 - 15 km | constrained EPZ; needs site-specific evacuation modelling |
| < 5 km | potentially exclusionary on EPZ grounds |

## Stage 3 implications

- Comfortable distance: Stage 3 confirms the city population from
  the latest census.
- Constrained distance: Stage 3 commissions a transport-route and
  evacuation-time-estimate (ETE) study coupled to RI-04 and EP-02.
- Inside 5 km: state that the EPZ design must be reconciled with
  the actual urban geometry before Stage 3 can recommend
  characterization.

## Do not

- Confuse city population with EPZ-radius population (RI-04 is the
  EPZ-radius metric).
- Imply that distance alone closes the question; topography and
  transport network shape evacuation feasibility.
