# Override notes - RI-04 Population Density at EPZ Radii

This override extends `03_radiological_impact.md` for Population
Density at EPZ Radii. Apply in addition to the family base prompt.

## Reading depth

Spend up to the family ceiling (~6 sentences). RI-04 carries one of
the highest weights in the composite and has four density radii plus
a total population count - the gradient is the analytic story.

## Required raw values to cite

- Population density within 5 km, 16 km, 25 km, 80 km (each /km2).
- Total population within 25 km (people).

## Plain-English reading

- Compare 5 km vs 25 km vs 80 km densities. A site with low 5 km
  density and high 80 km density sits inside a relatively empty
  pocket of an otherwise dense regional landscape - that is normal
  for many brownfield thermal sites.
- Reference the IAEA exclusion-area concept (low population zone in
  the immediate vicinity) and the ICRP / IAEA framing that emphasises
  the inner radii for prompt-action planning and the outer radii for
  long-term consequence.
- Total population at 25 km is the headline number for economic
  consequence modelling (MACCS-style). Quote it as a count of
  people, not a density.

## Stage 3 implications

- Stage 3 should refresh the GHSL POP raster against the latest
  national census.
- Stage 3 should run an MACCS or comparable consequence model with
  the host country's population projections to test long-term dose
  burden.
- Stage 3 should test the EPZ road and shelter capacity against the
  25 km population count (couple to EP-01 / EP-02).

## Do not

- Compare directly against US 10-mile / 50-mile EPZ thresholds; IAEA
  precautionary action zone (PAZ) and urgent protective action
  planning zone (UPZ) framing is the right reference.
- Imply a specific dose value from densities alone.
