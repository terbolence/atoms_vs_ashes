# Override notes - NS-08 Ecological Sensitivity

This override extends `05_non_safety_implementation.md` for
Ecological Sensitivity. Apply in addition to the family base prompt.

## Reading depth

Spend 4-6 sentences. Natura 2000 / WDPA proximity is the most common
permitting blocker for European brownfield sites and the criterion
most likely to set the EIA timeline.

## Required raw values to cite

- Natural land cover share (%).
- Distance to nearest Natura 2000 site (km).
- Distance to nearest WDPA area (km).
- Natura 2000 overlap flag.
- Natura 2000 sensitivity class.
- WDPA overlap flag.
- WDPA sensitivity class.
- Nearest Natura 2000 site name.
- Natura 2000 sites within 5 km (count).
- Nearest WDPA designation.

## Plain-English bands

| Natura 2000 overlap | Read |
| --- | --- |
| yes | potentially exclusionary; Article 6.3 appropriate assessment required |
| no, < 1 km | constrained; appropriate assessment likely |
| no, 1 - 5 km | workable; standard EIA scoping |
| no, > 5 km | comfortable; standard EIA scoping |

## Stage 3 implications

- Overlap: Stage 3 must run an EU Habitats Directive Article 6.3
  appropriate assessment and consult the European Commission where
  required.
- Adjacent (< 1 km): Stage 3 commissions an appropriate
  assessment, baseline biodiversity surveys (one full annual
  cycle), and stakeholder engagement with the conservation
  agency.
- Outside the buffer: Stage 3 commissions a standard EIA scoping
  with biodiversity baseline.
- Always state the nearest Natura 2000 site name and any WDPA
  designation; these are the names that will appear in any
  consenting brief.

## Do not

- Imply that EIA timing is binary; assessments often run 18-36
  months in EU jurisdictions.
- Promise mitigation acceptability.
- Recommend specific habitat-creation engineering.
