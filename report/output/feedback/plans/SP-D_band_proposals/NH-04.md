# Phase 0.6 — Band proposal: NH-04 Geotechnical — slope stability

## 1. Reviewer expectation

- **#94 (Timelkam, AT)**: anchor "Geotechnical: Slope Stability (NH-04)". Reviewer: "Score prea mic ca sa fie semnificativ trebuie sa afecteze amplsamentul nu la 1 km de amplasament si nu poate apare la un unghi de 10 grade" — "Score too low; to be significant the slope must affect the site itself, not 1 km away, and 10° cannot occur at the site."

Reviewer's mental model:
- The relevant slope statistic is the **mean slope under the footprint** (LL-019 fix), not the **maximum slope in a 1-km buffer**.
- A site with no real on-footprint slope problem (mean slope < 3°) should be in `[7,10]`.
- The exclusion threshold of 25° is reasonable, but `[3,4]` for "slope < 15°" is too punitive when on-footprint slopes are typically < 5°.

## 2. Current rubric (snapshot)

`primary_metric: slope_angle_deg`. The rubric reads **a single column** `slope_angle_deg` without specifying mean vs max — the connector populates it from a 1-km buffer max (per LL-019), which is the defect.

| Score range | Condition | Descriptor |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [9,10] | `slope_angle_deg < 1` | Optimal flat ground. |
| [7,8] | `slope_angle_deg < 3` | Gentle; minimal earthworks. |
| [5,6] | `slope_angle_deg < 8` | Moderate; routine grading; 5% cap at 5-6 boundary. |
| [3,4] | `slope_angle_deg < 15` | Significant slopes; stability study required. |
| [1,2] | `slope_angle_deg < 25` | Major instability risk; runout inventory must clear. |
| [0,0] | `slope_angle_deg >= 25` | E3 triggered. |

`fail_conditions`: E3 (exclude) on `slope >= 25 OR slope_stability_class == 'catastrophic'`.

## 3. Data sanity verdict

Phase 0.5 → `data_needs_fix_before_band_edit`. **Both** `nh04_gee_slope_max_deg` AND `nh04_gee_slope_mean_deg` are 100% NULL — the GEE slope columns were added per LL-019 but never populated by the connector. The rubric is currently reading `slope_angle_deg` (a different column populated from an older Overpass-based estimator).

## 4. Diagnosis

Three compounding defects:

1. **LL-019 connector defect**: the rubric reads `slope_angle_deg` (buffer max from old connector) instead of `slope_angle_mean_deg` (mean per LL-019). Fix is upstream in SP-F or a connector backfill.
2. **FB-LL-01 favorable-direction**: even with mean slope, the rubric `[5,6]` band catches `< 8°`, which the reviewer says is "moderate" — but at Timelkam mean slope is ~2° (gentle), so the criterion should be in `[7,8]` and is — verify.
3. **The reviewer comment #94 implicitly says "1 km buffer is wrong"**: this is LL-019, already documented. The rubric YAML cannot fix it alone; SP-D needs the connector to populate `slope_angle_mean_deg` and the rubric to consume it.

## 5. Proposed bands

**Two changes**: (a) re-target the primary metric to the **mean** (LL-019 fix) and document that the connector must populate it; (b) the band thresholds themselves do not need a numeric move, but the boundary descriptors are clarified.

| Score range | Condition (PROPOSED) | Delta vs current | Descriptor |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [9,10] | `slope_angle_mean_deg < 1 or (slope_angle_mean_deg is null and slope_angle_max_buffer_deg < 1)` | **CHANGE** primary metric to mean (LL-019); fall back to max if mean still missing | Optimal flat ground (mean < 1° on footprint). |
| [7,8] | `slope_angle_mean_deg < 3 or (slope_angle_mean_deg is null and slope_angle_max_buffer_deg < 3)` | same | Gentle; minimal earthworks (mean 1-3° on footprint). |
| [5,6] | `slope_angle_mean_deg < 8 or (slope_angle_mean_deg is null and slope_angle_max_buffer_deg < 8)` | same | Moderate; routine grading (mean 3-8° on footprint). |
| [3,4] | `slope_angle_mean_deg < 15 or (slope_angle_mean_deg is null and slope_angle_max_buffer_deg < 15)` | same | Significant slopes; stability study required (mean 8-15°). |
| [1,2] | `slope_angle_mean_deg < 25 or (slope_angle_mean_deg is null and slope_angle_max_buffer_deg < 25)` | same | Major instability risk (mean 15-25°). |
| [0,0] | `slope_angle_mean_deg >= 25 or slope_angle_max_buffer_deg >= 25` | E3 fires on **either** mean or max ≥ 25° (max is conservative for catastrophic landslide initiation polygons inside the buffer) | E3 triggered. |

**`fail_conditions` change**: E3 condition becomes `slope_angle_mean_deg >= 25 OR slope_angle_max_buffer_deg >= 25 OR slope_stability_class == 'catastrophic'`. **This widens the E3 exclusion population very slightly** — verify the count delta before commit.

`primary_metric:` field changes from `slope_angle_deg` → `slope_angle_mean_deg`.

## 8. Open questions for reviewer

- **Q-NH-04-A**: confirm `slope_angle_mean_deg` is the right primary metric (not `slope_angle_p90_deg` or some other percentile)? **Default-recommend mean per LL-019**.
