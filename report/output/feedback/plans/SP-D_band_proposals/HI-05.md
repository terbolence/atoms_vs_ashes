## 3. Data sanity verdict

Phase 0.5 → `data_needs_fix_before_band_edit`. `hazmat_route_distance_km` (= `nearest_hazmat_corridor_km`) is **100% NULL**. Connector is non-functional or not classifying corridors as hazmat.

## 4. Diagnosis

Same as HI-02 / HI-04. The hazmat-corridor classifier connector is missing or broken. Every site falls through to engine pass-mark 5.0.

**Additional concern**: even if the connector lands, the Stage-1 screening question is rarely "is there any hazmat corridor near the site" (almost every CEE site has *some* truck route within a few km), but rather "is there a **major** hazmat corridor (Trans-European Network road or class-1 rail with frequent hazmat traffic) within X km". Without corridor classification, even a populated `nearest_hazmat_corridor_km` value would over-report.

## 5. Proposed bands

| Score range | Condition (PROPOSED) | Delta | Descriptor |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [9,10] | `nearest_hazmat_corridor_km > 10 or (nearest_hazmat_corridor_km is null and hi05_search_completed == true)` | **ADD** sentinel | > 10 km (or no major hazmat corridor in search radius). |
| [7,8] | `nearest_hazmat_corridor_km >= 5` | unchanged | 5-10 km. |
| [5,6] | `nearest_hazmat_corridor_km >= 2` | unchanged | 2-5 km. |
| [3,4] | `nearest_hazmat_corridor_km >= 1` | unchanged | 1-2 km. |
| [1,2] | `nearest_hazmat_corridor_km < 1` | unchanged | < 1 km. |
| [0,0] | `nearest_hazmat_corridor_km < 0.1 and (has_pinch_point == true or has_pinch_point is null)` | NULL **NOT** conservative here — pinch_point is a positive-evidence flag, NULL = no evidence of pinch point | Unique pinch point. |

**Subtle**: for `[0,0]` we **DO NOT** treat NULL `has_pinch_point` as `true`. A pinch point is a positive-evidence flag (someone has to identify "this site has a unique single-route bottleneck"). NULL means "no evidence of pinch point", which should not trigger exclusion. This is the asymmetric NULL principle (FB-LL-08 v2) applied **the other way**: NULL on a positive-evidence-required flag stays neutral, not conservative-on-the-unfavorable-side.
