### Interpretation - Natural Hazards (NH)


Natural hazards at Bobov Dol are acceptable at screening level but carry the highest seismic loading among the selected Bulgarian sites. The 475-year PGA is 0.190 g and the 2,475-year PGA is 0.393 g. The nearest mapped capable fault is 11.1 km away, outside the exclusionary buffer but close enough for a site-specific seismic hazard review. Liquefaction susceptibility is moderate, bearing capacity is 88.7 kPa, depth to bedrock is 19.5 m, karst is not present, and the flood-zone class is negligible. Slope Stability (NH-04) remains unscored in the regenerated bundle, so Stage 3 should verify local slope and ground conditions alongside a probabilistic seismic hazard study.
