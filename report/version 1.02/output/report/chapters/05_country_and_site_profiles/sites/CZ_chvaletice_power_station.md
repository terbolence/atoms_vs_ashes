# Chvaletice power station Site Profile

It supports Stage 1-2 screening and defines the evidence needed before any Stage 3 judgement.

### Interpretation - Natural Hazards (NH)


Natural hazards are generally supportive at this screening stage. Ground-motion evidence is low, with PGA of 0.015 g at the 475-year return period and 0.034 g at the 2,475-year return period; the nearest mapped capable fault is 50.0 km away. Flooding and coastal exposure are not the leading constraints: the flood-zone class is negligible and the site is 406.2 km from the coast. Stage 3 should focus on Geotechnical: Subsidence (NH-05), Geotechnical: Foundation (NH-06), Extreme Winds (NH-10), with particular attention to screening wind speed, mine or foundation evidence, and local hydrology where the bundle marks data quality below final-design standard.

Human-induced hazards are the binding avoidance issue for this site. Aircraft Crash (HI-01) is flagged because the nearest recorded airport or heliport is 5.05 km away and the nearest flight-path proxy is 2.53 km away.
