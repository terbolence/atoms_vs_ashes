# Consolidated Failure Section - Countries with No Exclusionary-Pass Site

This section consolidates the countries whose entire thermal-station portfolio is removed at the exclusionary screen against the NuScale VOYGR-6 reference envelope. None of these countries currently support a brownfield coal-to-nuclear conversion path from the screened candidate pool; any future programme would require a greenfield prospecting effort that applies the same exclusionary rules at the screening stage.

The criterion codes referenced below resolve as follows:

- **NH-02** Seismic: Surface Rupture
- **NH-03** Volcanic Hazard
- **NH-04** Geotechnical: Slope Stability
- **EP-01** Emergency Planning Feasibility

## Country roll

- **Albania (AL)** - 1 of 1 site removed at the exclusionary screen. Porto Romano Power Station fails NH-02 and NH-03, reflecting the regional Adriatic capable-fault context and proximity to volcanic hazard sources. The country has no further brownfield candidate to fall back to.
- **Slovenia (SI)** - 3 of 3 sites removed at the exclusionary screen. Sostanj power station and Trbovlje power station fail NH-02 (the latter also fails NH-04 on slope stability); Te-Tol power station fails NH-02 and EP-01. The result is structural: the Slovenian thermal-station footprint sits on or adjacent to capable-fault traces in the Sava and Savinja valleys.
- **Kosovo (XK)** - 4 of 4 sites removed at the exclusionary screen. All four sites fail NH-02 (Istok, Kosovo A, Kosovo B, Kosovo C); Kosovo C also fails EP-01. The Kosovan thermal cluster is concentrated in the Drenica and Obilic basin, which sits within the Vardar Zone seismic context.

## Implications

This section is a stub for the writing pass that follows the country profiles. The full narrative will:

- Quote the actual hazard values that drive the NH-02 / NH-03 / NH-04 fails for each site (PGA at 475-yr return, capable-fault distance, slope-stability factor of safety) once the per-site evidence is pulled from the bundle.
- Explain why a greenfield search inside each country must apply the regional capable-fault buffer at the screening stage rather than at characterization.
- Discuss whether cross-border programme coordination (Albania - Kosovo, Slovenia - Croatia) is a credible alternative to a domestic greenfield search.

The sites in this section are not deal-breakers for nuclear ambition in the host country; they are deal-breakers for the brownfield coal-to-nuclear path on the existing thermal portfolio. The distinction matters when host governments consider whether to launch a greenfield site-survey programme alongside the regional fleet plan.
