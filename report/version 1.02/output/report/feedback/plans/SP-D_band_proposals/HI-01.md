## 3. Data sanity verdict

Phase 0.5 → `criterion_blocked_until_connector_rework`. The current connector emits `nearest_airport_km` but **does not classify airport type** (small vs large vs military) consistently — `nearest_airport_type` is sparsely populated and the values used in the rubric (`'large_intl'`, `'military_major'`) do not match any documented enum. **The fundamental defect is FB-LL-03**: distance alone cannot honor the reviewer's mental model.

## 4. Diagnosis

Three compounding defects:

1. **FB-LL-08**: `[9,10]` band requires `nearest_airport_km > 30 AND nearest_military_airfield_km > 60`. With `nearest_military_airfield_km` frequently NULL, the AND fails and favourable sites drop to `[5,6]`.
2. **FB-LL-03**: distance-only scoring conflates "small airfield at 9 km" (low risk) with "major hub at 9 km" (high risk). Reviewer #76, #79, #106, #578 all hammer this point.
3. **Enum mismatch**: rubric references `nearest_airport_type IN ['small_ga', 'general_aviation', 'large_intl', 'military_major', ...]` but the connector populates only `'commercial'`, `'small_airport'`, etc. — no consistent vocabulary.
