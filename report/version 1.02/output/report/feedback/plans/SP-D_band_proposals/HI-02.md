For Timelkam (AT, no Seveso facility within 20 km of the site coordinates):
- `[9,10]`: `nearest_seveso_km > 20` — `(NULL > 20)` is NULL/false → NO.
- All bands: NO.
- → engine pass-mark 5.0; renderer says "values not in measurement tables".

Reviewer #107: "score too low when you have not found data" — this is the FB-LL-02 credibility failure. Reviewer #579 says the same thing more bluntly.

## 5. Proposed bands

The pattern is identical to NH-07 (Volcanism): add a sentinel `hi02_search_completed: bool` field; the connector writes `true` when the Seveso registry was successfully queried and returned no facility within the search radius (e.g. 20 km).

| Score range | Condition (PROPOSED) | Delta vs current | Descriptor |

| [9,10] | `nearest_seveso_km > 20 or (nearest_seveso_km is null and hi02_search_completed == true)` | **ADD** OR-clause: NULL + connector-confirmed-search → favorable | > 20 km (or no Seveso facility in search radius). |

| [5,6] | `nearest_seveso_km >= 5` | unchanged | 5-10 km (project A7 pass-mark). |
