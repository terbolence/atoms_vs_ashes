# Phase 0.6 — Band proposal: NH-05 Subsidence / karst / mining / oil & gas

## 1. Reviewer expectation

- **#95 (Timelkam, AT)**: anchor "- score 5.5". Reviewer: "Score prea mic – asta inseamna tasari mari sau cavitati in teren pe amplasament" — "Score too low — that means large settlement or voids in the ground on-site."

Reviewer's mental model: 5.5 implies the system has detected actual subsidence/voids on the site footprint. If the data says `karst=none` and no mining void within 10 km, the score should be `[9,10]`.

## 2. Current rubric (snapshot)

`primary_metric: mining_void_distance_km`. Conditions already partially handle NULL via `in [null, ...]` clauses.

| Score range | Condition | Descriptor |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [9,10] | `karst_severity == 'none' and mining_void_distance_km >= 10 and subsidence_risk_class in [null, 'none']` | No karst; nearest mapped mine feature well beyond score-5 boundary; subsidence risk none/unknown. |
| [7,8] | `karst_severity in [null, 'none'] and mining_void_distance_km >= 4 and subsidence_risk_class in [null, 'none', 'low']` | No karst on site; clear mine-feature distance margin; low/unknown subsidence risk. |
| [5,6] | `karst_severity in [null, 'none', 'moderate'] and (mining_void_distance_km is null or mining_void_distance_km >= 2) and subsidence_risk_class in [null, 'none', 'low', 'moderate', 'high']` | Possible with geotechnical confirmation; mapped mine feature at/above acceptance pivot or unknown. |
| [3,4] | `mining_void_distance_km >= 1 and mining_void_distance_km < 2 and karst_severity != 'high'` | Below mine-distance pivot but not extreme; geotechnical review required. |
| [1,2] | `mining_void_distance_km < 1 or karst_severity == 'high'` | Close mine feature or high karst proxy; severe review signal, no exclusion without site-specific evidence. |

`fail_conditions`: E5 / E6 are review-only (not exclusionary).

## 3. Data sanity verdict

Phase 0.5 → `data_needs_fix_before_band_edit`. `subsidence_risk_class` is **100% NULL**; `mining_void_distance_km` is 72.3% NULL; `karst_severity` populated.

## 4. Diagnosis

The `[9,10]` band requires `karst_severity == 'none' AND mining_void_distance_km >= 10`. With `mining_void_distance_km` NULL on 72.3% of sites, the AND-clause fails for most sites. Even though the rubric **does** allow `mining_void_distance_km IS NULL` in the `[5,6]` and `[7,8]` bands, it does **not** allow it in `[9,10]`. This is the FB-LL-01 + FB-LL-08 pattern.

For Timelkam (AT, `karst_severity = 'none'`, `mining_void_distance_km = NULL`, `subsidence_risk_class = NULL`):
- `[9,10]`: requires `mining_void_distance_km >= 10` — NO (NULL).
- `[7,8]`: requires `mining_void_distance_km >= 4` — NO (NULL).
- `[5,6]`: matches (karst in [null, 'none','moderate'] AND mining_void_distance_km is null AND subsidence_risk_class in [null,...]) → 5.5.

Score: 5.5 with quality medium. Reviewer reads "5.5/10" as "system thinks there's subsidence here", when the system intended "we have no mine-distance data and the karst data is favorable, so we default to pass-mark". FB-LL-02 + FB-LL-01 combined.

## 5. Proposed bands

| Score range | Condition (PROPOSED) | Delta vs current | Descriptor |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [9,10] | `karst_severity == 'none' and (mining_void_distance_km >= 10 or (mining_void_distance_km is null and country_mining_history_score <= 1)) and subsidence_risk_class in [null, 'none']` | **ADD** OR-clause for "no mining history at country/region level" via a country baseline lookup | No karst; no nearby mining (or no regional mining history); subsidence risk none/unknown. |
| [7,8] | `karst_severity in [null, 'none'] and (mining_void_distance_km >= 4 or (mining_void_distance_km is null and country_mining_history_score <= 2)) and subsidence_risk_class in [null, 'none', 'low']` | same OR-clause | No karst on site; clear mine-feature distance margin (or no regional mining history). |

`primary_metric:` unchanged. The `country_mining_history_score` is a **proposed new field** in `country_baseline` (1 = no historic mining; 2 = limited; 3 = significant; 4 = extensive). For the EU regional set, this can be authored manually from EGDI / national mining cadasters with very low effort (~1 hour).

## 8. Open questions for reviewer
