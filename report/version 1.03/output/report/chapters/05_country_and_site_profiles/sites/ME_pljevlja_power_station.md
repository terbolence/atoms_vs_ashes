## Why It Failed

The site fails 2 exclusionary criteria below. Exclusionary failures act as gates: a single confirmed failure removes the site from the brownfield candidate pool until the underlying measurement is refuted by site-specific Stage 3 work or until a regulatory threshold change makes the failure moot.

| Ecological Sensitivity | NS-08 | Strict protected-area overlap: yes | Strict protected-area or international-designation overlap | low | Triggered by protected-area overlap. |

## Unlock Analysis


Pljevlja fails on two exclusionary checks. Seismic: Surface Rupture (NH-02) records the nearest mapped capable fault at 0.09 km, effectively adjacent to the site boundary, and Ecological Sensitivity (NS-08) records a strict protected-area overlap. The combined finding is structural: one trigger concerns the physical fault setting, while the other concerns the protected-area envelope. Either item would remove the site from the brownfield candidate pool; together they make site-level recovery implausible without a different site boundary and a successful protected-area review. No further site-level characterisation is justified for the current power-station footprint. **Recommendation: Deprecate.**

## Evidence Limitations

- This profile is intentionally compact. Composite scoring, Monte Carlo stability, family contributions, and the residual risk register are omitted because none of those views are informative for a site that is removed at the exclusionary screen.
- The exclusionary thresholds are screening thresholds; site-specific Stage 3 measurement can either confirm the failure or lift it. The unlock analysis above states whether that investment is justified.
