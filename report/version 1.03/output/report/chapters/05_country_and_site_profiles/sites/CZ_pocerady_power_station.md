### Interpretation - Natural Hazards (NH)


Natural hazards are generally supportive at this screening stage. Ground-motion evidence is low, with PGA of 0.017 g at the 475-year return period and 0.034 g at the 2,475-year return period; the nearest mapped capable fault is 50.0 km away. Flooding and coastal exposure are not the leading constraints: the flood-zone class is negligible and the site is 362.9 km from the coast. Stage 3 should focus on Geotechnical: Subsidence (NH-05), Geotechnical: Foundation (NH-06), Extreme Winds (NH-10), with particular attention to screening wind speed, mine or foundation evidence, and local hydrology where the bundle marks data quality below final-design standard.
