# Adamow power station Site Profile

Adamow power station is a coal/thermal site in Poland that has been tested against the NuScale VOYGR-6 reference deployment envelope. This profile reads the screening evidence at a level that supports a decision to progress toward Stage 3 characterisation. It is not a site-suitability determination, vendor recommendation, or licensing finding.

### Interpretation - Natural Hazards (NH)


Natural hazards at Adamow are generally favourable, with liquefaction, wind, and flood confirmation the practical Stage 3 priorities. Ground motion is very low, with PGA of 0.011 g at the 475-year return period and 0.029 g at the 2,475-year return period, and no mapped capable fault is recorded within 50.0 km. Liquefaction susceptibility is high on loamy sand, while bearing capacity is 120.0 kPa and depth to bedrock is 24.8 m. Karst is absent, coastal distance is 261.6 km, and the flood-zone class is negligible, but River Flooding (NH-09) still carries insufficient data quality. Extreme Winds (NH-10) is weaker at 3.5/10 with screening wind-speed index of 11.0 m/s. Stage 3 should therefore confirm foundation behaviour, liquefaction susceptibility, local flood inputs, and wind loading before detailed layout selection.

### Interpretation - Non-Safety and Implementation Considerations (NS)


Adamow's implementation case benefits from a retired brownfield setting but carries cooling-water, land-development, and transport-data questions. The canonical site surface area is 158.9 ha, while the development-area estimate is 6.0 ha; the latter should be treated only as a screening-stage development envelope. Cooling Water Availability (NS-01) scores 4.0/10 because the Kiełbaska is 5.08 km away, recorded flow is 0.73 m3/s, and water stress is High. Grid evidence is plausible, with a substation 0.3 km away, a high-voltage line 0.15 km away, 110.0 kV highest nearby voltage, and 600.0 MW export capacity. Site Topography (NS-04) is strong, with 88.8% favourable land cover and 211.8 ha favourable area. Stage 3 should test whether cooling water, grid reinforcement, developable land, and transport access can support a NuScale VOYGR-6 envelope.
