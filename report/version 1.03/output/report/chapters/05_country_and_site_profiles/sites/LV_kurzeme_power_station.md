This profile reads the screening evidence at a level that supports a decision to progress toward Stage 3 characterisation. It is not a site-suitability determination, vendor recommendation, or licensing finding.

### Interpretation - Natural Hazards (NH)


Natural hazards are the strongest part of the Kurzeme screening profile, but they are not a clean-pass story. Seismic evidence is favourable: PGA is 0.018 g at the 475-year return period and 0.068 g at the 2,475-year return period, while the nearest mapped capable fault is recorded at 50.0 km with no slip-rate signal. The geotechnical read is moderate and suitable for Stage 3 confirmation rather than final design: liquefaction susceptibility is moderate, bearing capacity is 97.0 kPa, depth to bedrock is 9.53 m, and the mean site slope is 3.54 degrees. The main natural-hazard constraints are coastal and meteorological. Coastal Flooding (NH-08) scores 3.5/10 because the site is 2.17 km from the coast at 9.95 m elevation, and Extreme Winds (NH-10) scores 1.5/10 on a 14.4 m/s design wind-speed proxy. River Flooding (NH-09) is marked negligible but still carries insufficient data quality. Stage 3 should therefore open with Baltic coastal inundation and storm-surge modelling, project-specific wind extreme-value analysis, and a geotechnical campaign to confirm liquefaction, bearing and foundation inputs.

### Interpretation - Human-Induced and Security-Relevant Hazards (HI)


Human-induced and security-relevant hazards are the main reason Kurzeme remains an avoidance-flag candidate. Aircraft Crash (HI-01) scores 3.5/10 because Ventspils International Airport is 6.49 km from the site, the nearest flight-path proxy is 3.24 km away, and one airport is recorded within the search radius. Military Installations (HI-06) is more severe, scoring 0.0/10: the nearest military feature is 4.21 km away, the nearest high-consequence depot-class feature is 4.7 km away, and 137 military features are recorded within the screening radius. Electromagnetic Interference (HI-07) also needs early attention, with the nearest transmitter at 0.33 km and 194 transmitter-like features within the search radius. The wider industrial-hazard record is more favourable: the nearest industrial and toxic-source distances are both 16.3 km, and the external-fire row records no measured in-radius value. Stage 3 should therefore begin with an aircraft-crash hazard assessment, defence/security feature confirmation, and a measured RF/EMI survey before the site is advanced as a full-pass brownfield candidate.

Stage 3 should model EPZ time-to-clear for Ventspils and the rural coastal road network, then run site-specific meteorological and liquid-pathway modelling for the Venta and Baltic coastal setting.

### Interpretation - Non-Safety and Implementation Considerations (NS)


Non-safety and implementation considerations give Kurzeme its main brownfield value and its main programme constraints. Cooling Water Availability (NS-01) is strong: the Venta is 1.57 km away, the recorded flow is 128.8 m<sup>3</sup>/s, and the water-stress label is Low. Transport access is also strong, with a highway at 0.12 km, rail at 0.36 km, a waterway at 2.84 km, and heavy-haul capability recorded. Grid Connection (NS-02) is the first implementation constraint: the nearest substation is 0.26 km away and the nearest high-voltage line is 1.03 km away, but the highest nearby line voltage is 110 kV and the grid export-capacity estimate is 435 MW. Land is the second constraint. The canonical site surface area is 8.7 ha, and the current available-development figure is also 8.7 ha; the 32.5 ha favourable-area value is a wider screening-stage expansion envelope and does not prove controlled, contiguous or permitted development land. Ecological Sensitivity (NS-08) is another Stage 3 issue because Būšnieku ezera krasts is 3.851 km away, one Natura 2000 site lies within 5 km, and the nearest protected-area designation is a Nature Reserve. Stage 3 should therefore confirm the site boundary and land-control envelope, test the grid-export pathway for the NuScale VOYGR-6 reference case, and complete Natura 2000 and national EIA scoping.
