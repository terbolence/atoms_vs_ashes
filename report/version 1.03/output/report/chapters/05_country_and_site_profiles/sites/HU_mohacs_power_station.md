### Interpretation - Natural Hazards (NH)

Natural hazards at Mohacs are favourable for Stage 1-2 screening, with the main uncertainty concentrated in local geotechnical and flood confirmation rather than in a standing exclusionary trigger. The 475-year PGA is 0.054 g and the 2,475-year PGA is 0.121 g, while the nearest mapped capable fault is 50.0 km from the site. Liquefaction remains a field question because the screening layer records high susceptibility on silty clay loam, but the foundation proxy is moderate at 91.3 kPa bearing capacity and 21.5 m depth to bedrock. The site is 315.6 km from the coast, the river-flood class is negligible at screening level, the screening wind-speed index is 7.43 m/s, and the temperature envelope runs from -4.51 deg C to 25.53 deg C. Stage 3 should start with Danube design-basis flood confirmation, CPT/SPT geotechnical testing and a local precipitation and drainage review.
