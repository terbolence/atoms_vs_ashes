# Document Map: IAEA SSG-79
**Title:** Hazards Associated with Human Induced External Events in Site Evaluation for Nuclear Installations  
**Series:** IAEA Safety Standards Series No. SSG-79 (Specific Safety Guide)  
**Year:** 2023 | **Pages:** 114 | **Supersedes:** NS-G-3.1 (2002)

---

## Purpose
Provides updated recommendations on evaluating hazards from human induced external events (accidental origin) at nuclear installation sites to meet SSR-1 Requirement 24. Supersedes the widely-referenced NS-G-3.1 with modernised guidance.

---

## Section Map

### Section 1: Introduction
- Supersedes NS-G-3.1 (2002). Updated to reflect current industrial landscape and transport evolution.
- Covers accidental events only (not malicious acts, which fall under IAEA Nuclear Security Series).
- Applies to all nuclear installations.

### Section 2: General Recommendations
- Systematic identification of all human induced hazards within the region.
- Consider both existing and foreseeable future activities over plant lifetime.
- Screening approach: assess whether hazard warrants detailed analysis based on distance and magnitude.

### Section 3: Identification of Sources
- **Stationary sources:**
  - Industrial facilities (chemical plants, refineries, petrochemical, munitions)
  - Oil and gas operations
  - Mining and quarrying
  - Military installations (permanent/temporary), shooting ranges, arsenals
  - Other nuclear installations
  - High-energy rotating equipment
  - Broadcasting/communication (EMI)
  - Hydraulic structures (dams, reservoirs)
- **Mobile sources:**
  - Road transport (trucks with hazardous cargo)
  - Rail transport (tank cars, freight)
  - Maritime/inland waterway (ships, barges)
  - Pipeline transport (oil, gas, chemicals)
  - Air transport (civilian and military aircraft, flight paths, airports)

### Section 4: Hazard Assessment Methods
- **Aircraft crash analysis:** Probability estimation using traffic data, crash rates. Screening distances: 4–16 km from airports (per SSG-35 Table II-1).
- **Explosion analysis:** TNT equivalence, overpressure vs. distance calculations. Screening distance: 5–8 km from facilities.
- **Toxic/flammable cloud analysis:** Gaussian dispersion models, source term estimation. Screening distance: 8 km from sources.
- **Fire analysis:** Heat flux calculations, flame impingement assessment.
- **Missile/impact analysis:** Vehicle impact, industrial projectiles.
- **Electromagnetic interference:** Assessment of interference with I&C systems.

### Section 5: Screening Criteria and Design Basis
- Screening probability criterion: typically 10⁻⁷/year per event.
- If screening shows hazard probability > threshold, detailed analysis required.
- Design basis parameters: overpressure, impact force, heat flux, toxic concentration.

## Key Screening Distances (consistent with SSG-35 Table II-1)
| Hazard Source | Screening Distance |
|---|---|
| Flight paths approaching airport | 4.0 km |
| Airport (Type 2 event) | 7.5 km |
| Small airports | 10.0 km |
| Large airports | 16.0 km |
| Military ranges/bombing areas | 30.0 km |
| Military ammunition storage | 8.0 km |
| Hazmat storage/processing | 5.0 km |
| Hazardous cloud sources | 8.0 km |
