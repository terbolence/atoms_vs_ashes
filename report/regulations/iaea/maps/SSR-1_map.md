# Document Map: IAEA SSR-1
**Title:** Site Evaluation for Nuclear Installations  
**Series:** IAEA Safety Standards Series No. SSR-1 (Specific Safety Requirements)  
**Year:** 2019 | **Pages:** 56 | **Supersedes:** NS-R-3 (Rev. 1, 2016), NS-R-3 (2003)

---

## Purpose
Establishes mandatory safety REQUIREMENTS for site evaluation for nuclear installations. This is the overarching requirements document — all Safety Guides (SSG-35, SSG-9, SSG-18, SSG-21, SSG-79, etc.) provide recommendations on HOW to meet these requirements. Uses "shall" statements.

---

## Section Map

### Section 1: Introduction (pp. 1–4, §1.1–1.18)
- Supersedes NS-R-3 (Rev. 1); incorporates post-Fukushima lessons.
- Applies to all nuclear installations (NPPs, research reactors, fuel cycle facilities, spent fuel storage).
- Covers both new and existing installations.
- Siting process: site survey (identify candidates) → site selection (screen, evaluate, rank).

### Section 2: Safety Principles and Concepts (pp. 5–6, §2.1–2.6)
- **Requirement 1 — Safety objective:** Characterize natural and human induced external hazards to provide adequate input for demonstrating protection of people and the environment.

### Section 3: Management System (pp. 7, §3.1–3.5)
- **Requirement 2:** Apply management system throughout site evaluation.

### Section 4: General Requirements for Site Evaluation (pp. 8–17, §4.1–4.50)
- **Requirement 3 — Scope (§4.1–4.5):** All hazards must be evaluated. Site evaluation extends through entire installation lifetime.
- **Requirement 4 — Site suitability (§4.6–4.11):** Site deemed unsuitable if no engineering solutions exist for identified hazards. Must consider combined effects of hazards.
- **Requirement 5 — Site and regional characteristics (§4.12–4.15):** Define region size per hazard type. Investigate all features relevant to safety.
- **Requirement 6 — Identification of hazards (§4.16–4.19):** Comprehensive identification of all natural and human induced hazards.
- **Requirement 7 — Evaluation of external hazards (§4.20–4.28):** Both PSHA and DSHA approaches. Consider hazard combinations. Use conservative approach where data is limited. Account for uncertainties.
- **Requirement 8 — Site protection measures (§4.29–4.31):** Protection measures shall be evaluated for their effectiveness.
- **Requirement 9 — Multiple installations (§4.32–4.33):** Evaluate combined risk from multiple installations on same/adjacent sites.
- **Requirement 10 — Changes with time (§4.34–4.35):** Consider climate change, land use changes, population growth over installation lifetime.
- **Requirement 11 — Ultimate heat sink (§4.36–4.37):** Evaluate reliability of heat sink under all conditions.
- **Requirement 12 — Effects on people/environment (§4.38–4.40):** Assess radiological impact on population and environment.
- **Requirement 13 — Emergency response feasibility (§4.41–4.43):** Confirm emergency plans are feasible for the site.
- **Requirement 14 — Data collection (§4.44–4.50):** Systematic, transparent, retrievable data collection. GIS recommended.

### Section 5: Evaluation of External Hazards (pp. 17–25, §5.1–5.37)
- **Requirement 15 — Fault capability (§5.2–5.4):** Evaluate all faults of significant size within defined distance. Identify capable faults.
- **Requirement 16 — Ground motion (§5.5):** Determine vibratory ground motion hazards.
- **Requirement 17 — Volcanic hazards (§5.6–5.10):** Screen and evaluate volcanic hazards. Site deemed unsuitable if volcanic hazards cannot be managed.
- **Requirement 18 — Extreme meteorological hazards (§5.11–5.12):** Evaluate extreme values of meteorological parameters.
- **Requirement 19 — Rare meteorological events (§5.13–5.14):** Evaluate tornadoes, tropical cyclones, other rare events.
- **Requirement 20 — Flooding hazards (§5.15–5.23):** Evaluate all flooding sources (river, coastal, tsunami, dam break, precipitation). Consider combinations.
- **Requirement 21 — Geotechnical characteristics (§5.24–5.26):** Characterize subsurface materials.
- **Requirement 22 — Geotechnical hazards (§5.27–5.31):** Evaluate liquefaction, slope instability, subsidence, collapse, karst. Site unsuitable if no engineering remedy.
- **Requirement 23 — Other natural hazards (§5.32):** Evaluate biological hazards, wildfire, etc.
- **Requirement 24 — Human induced events (§5.33–5.37):** Evaluate aircraft crash, explosions, toxic releases, fires, transport hazards, electromagnetic interference.

### Section 6: Effects on the Region (pp. 25–27, §6.1–6.11)
- **Requirement 25 — Dispersion (§6.1–6.7):** Evaluate atmospheric, surface water, and groundwater dispersion pathways.
- **Requirement 26 — Population (§6.8–6.10):** Assess population distribution and projected public exposure.
- **Requirement 27 — Land and water use (§6.11):** Evaluate current and projected land/water use.

### Section 7: Monitoring and Periodic Review (pp. 28–29, §7.1–7.5)
- **Requirement 28 — Monitoring (§7.1–7.3):** Continuous monitoring of external hazards.
- **Requirement 29 — Review (§7.4–7.5):** Periodic review of site conditions and hazards.
