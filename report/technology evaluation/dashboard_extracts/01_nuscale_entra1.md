# NuScale ENTRA1 Energy Plant — Evidence Corpus

**Vendor / operator pairing:** NuScale Power Corporation (technology) + ENTRA1 Energy LLC (project development)
**Reference design configuration:** 6 × 77 MWe NuScale Power Modules (NPMs), gross **462 MWe** (VOYGR-6)
**Coolant / spectrum:** Light water / thermal
**Fuel:** UO₂ ceramic, U-235 enrichment < 5%, HTP2 design
**Romania project:** RoPower Nuclear S.A. (50/50 SNN + Nova Power & Gas) at the **Doicești** former coal-plant site
**Doicești COD target:** 2033 (per Feb 2026 FID)

## 1. NEA SMR Dashboard 3rd Edition extract

> **Reactor description:** Integral multi-module pressurised water reactor.
> Thermal power: 250 MWth per module — Core outlet temperature: 321 °C — Spectrum: thermal — Fuel form: Uranium-oxide ceramic — Base fissile material: U-235, enrichment < 5%.

(NEA Dashboard 3rd Ed (2025), p. 198.)

> **Licensing.** ENTRA1 Energy Plants™ with NuScale SMRs-inside (ENTRA1 Energy Plants) can be configured with 4, 6 or 12 NuScale Power Modules (NPM) of 77 MWe each, providing 308 MWe (4 NPMs), 462 MWe (6 NPMs), and 924 MWe (12 NPM), respectively. In 2020, the US Nuclear Regulatory Commission (NRC) issued a Standard Design Approval (SDA) for the 50 MWe NPM in a 12-NPM configuration and issued its final rule in February 2023. In July 2023, the NRC docketed for review NuScale's SDA application for the 77 MWe NPM uprate, with a mutually agreed upon 24-month review period. NuScale has also been conducting pre-licensing activities in Poland, Romania and Ukraine.

> **Siting.** NuScale continues to progress in Romania, where RoPower, a joint venture of Nuclearelectrica and Nova Power & Gas, contracted NuScale for engineering and site evaluation of an ENTRA1 Energy Plant with a 6-NPM configuration at Doicești. Romania's nuclear regulator approved the project's Licensing Basis Document in 2023 and in 2024, an IAEA mission confirmed the site meets international safety standards. That same year, Nuclearelectrica and RoPower signed a Phase 2 engineering contract with Fluor Corporation. Also in 2024, NuScale advanced in the United Kingdom's Great British Nuclear selection process.

> **Financing.** NuScale has been awarded matching funds from the US Department of Energy (DOE): in 2013, USD 226 million (USD 2023 295 million); and in 2020, USD 1.355 billion (USD 2023 1.595 billion) over ten years. NuScale became publicly traded in 2022 and received USD 380 million (USD 2023 396 million) in the transaction. In 2023, Japan, Korea, the United Arab Emirates and the United States announced up to USD 2023 275 million in new financing. In 2023, export-import banks in Korea and the United States expressed their interest in supporting NuScale deployment. In 2024, NuScale received approximately USD 228 million (USD 2023 221 million) after redemption of all its outstanding warrants.

> **Supply chain.** NuScale has developed a mature supply chain through agreements with Framatome, Ultra Electronics, Sarens, Samsung C&T Corporation, Doosan Enerbility, PaR Systems, and Concurrent Technologies. NuScale has advanced testing, engineering, and review capabilities through agreements including with SIET, the US Reactor Forging Consortium, Oregon State University, Sargent and Lundy, Enercon, and BWXT. Through a strategic alliance with NuScale, ENTRA1 develops, finances, owns and operates ENTRA1 Energy Plants with NuScale SMRs-inside. In 2023, Doosan started forging upper reactor pressure vessels for NuScale. In 2025, Doosan ordered steam generator tubes from Alleima. NuScale was also selected for a Project-Working Group under the European Industrial Alliance on SMRs.

> **Engagement.** As of early 2025, NuScale had deployed eight Energy Exploration Centers (E2 Centers) in Africa, Asia, Europe, and the United States as educational tools in the use of modular reactors. NuScale has also engaged communities through town hall meetings. In 2023, the Polish Ministry of Climate and Environment issued a Decision-In-Principle to support deployment in Poland of ENTRA1 Energy Plants with NuScale SMRs-inside. NuScale joined the United Nations 24/7 Carbon-free Energy Compact, a group of stakeholders committed to carbon-free energy solutions. In Korea, GS Energy and the Uljin County signed an MoU to conduct feasibility studies of using ENTRA1 Energy Plants for hydrogen production.

> **Fuel.** In 2016, NuScale included its proprietary HTP2 fuel in its application to the NRC for design certification of the 50 MWe NPM in a 12-NPM ENTRA1 Energy Plant configuration. In 2020, the NRC issued an SDA for the 50 MWe NPM in said configuration and issued its final rule in February 2023, approving by incorporation by reference the use of the HTP2 fuel design. HTP2 fuel can be fabricated at Framatome's fabrication facilities in the United States and Europe and uses proven Framatome components with extensive operating history. HTP2 fuel is similar to fuel used in operating commercial nuclear plants and will not require further development. In 2015, NuScale Power and Areva (now Framatome) signed a contract to manufacture the fuel assemblies for the initial core and reloads.

(NEA Dashboard 3rd Ed (2025), pp. 198-199.)

## 2. April 2026 evidence snapshot

### Doicești project status (Romania)

- **12 February 2026:** Nuclearelectrica (SNN) shareholders approved the **Final Investment Decision (FID)** for the Doicești 6×NPM SMR project, transitioning from the analysis phase to implementation. ([SNN press release 2026-02-12](https://nuclearelectrica.ro/snn/en/2026/02/12/the-doicesti-small-modular-reactors-smr-project-obtains-the-final-investment-decision-and-enters-the-third-stage-of-development/); [WNN 2026-02](https://www.world-nuclear-news.org/articles/final-investment-decision-taken-for-romanias-smrs))
- **Total project cost:** **USD 4.9 billion ±20–30%** for the 6-NPM configuration. The Romanian PM has cited a higher USD 6–7 billion figure including site infrastructure and contingencies. ([Romania-Insider 2026-02](https://www.romania-insider.com/nuclearelectrica-approves-doicestismr-project-feb-2026); [WNN 2026-02](https://www.world-nuclear-news.org/articles/final-investment-decision-taken-for-romanias-smrs))
- **Implementation gating condition:** Romania has committed to fund **only the first 77 MWe module initially**; the remaining five modules will proceed only after the first is proven to function properly. ([Power Magazine 2026-02](https://www.powermag.com/romanias-coal-to-nuscale-smr-conversion-secures-fid-moves-into-implementation-with-caveats/))
- **Stage 3 (Pre-EPC):** runs **15 months until mid-2027** with an allocated budget of ~**USD 600 million**. By May 2026, RoPower Nuclear is to finalise EPC contract negotiations. ([NEI Magazine 2026-02](https://www.neimagazine.com/news/fid-for-doicesti-smr/))
- **COD target unit 1:** **2033**, with subsequent five units staggered through ~2036. ([Neutron Bytes 2026-02-13](https://neutronbytes.com/2026/02/13/final-investment-decision-approved-for-six-nuscale-smrs-in-romania/))
- **Confidential FID conditions** (Annexes 3 and 6): support frameworks, partnerships, mitigation of potential gas leaks at the site. ([Romania-Insider 2026-02](https://www.romania-insider.com/nuclearelectrica-approves-doicestismr-project-feb-2026))

### NRC Standard Design Approval

- **29 May 2025:** NRC **issued Standard Design Approval (SDA) for the NuScale US460 (77 MWe NPM)** design. This is the SDA that the Doicești project deploys. ([NuScale press release 2025-05-29](https://www.nuscalepower.com/press-releases/2025/nuscale-powers-small-modular-reactor-smr-achieves-standard-design-approval-from-us-nuclear-regulatory-commission-for-77-mwe); [NRC 25-033](https://www.nrc.gov/cdn/doc-collection-news/2025/25-033.pdf); [NRC project page](https://www.nrc.gov/reactors/new-reactors/advanced/who-were-working-with/applicant-projects/nuscale-us460))
- The 77 MWe SDA explicitly supersedes the 2020/2023 50 MWe SDA for new deployments.

### EU / European deployment context

- NuScale was selected for a **Project-Working Group under the EU Industrial Alliance on SMRs** in 2024.
- Polish **Decision-in-Principle** (2023) for ENTRA1 deployment remains active but no PSB Energa or Industria Polska has yet committed siting funds for NuScale.
- UK GBN competition: NuScale was eliminated in 2024 (Rolls-Royce SMR was selected; Holtec, BWRX-300 and one other are still alive in subsequent procurement waves).

## 4. Open data gaps

- **Per-MWe overnight CAPEX disclosure for Doicești** is opaque: the USD 4.9B headline mixes EPC, owner's costs, IDC, infrastructure, and contingency. The model uses 4.9B / 462 MWe = ~10,600 USD/kWe FOAK as a base, with low/high sensitivity 8,500 / 13,500 USD/kWe.
- **Learning-rate evidence for NuScale** is weak (no completed second VOYGR plant). The model uses a default 8 % per doubling, with 4 % low / 12 % high.
- **Cooling configuration** at Doicești (river / cooling tower / hybrid) is published only at a high level — the LBD allows multiple options. Assumed: hybrid with mechanical-draft cooling towers (water-constrained Argeș basin) for Tier B B5.
- **First-fuel delivery contract**: confirmed Framatome supplier but specific delivery dates for Doicești initial core not in public domain.
- **EPZ (Emergency Planning Zone) decision under CNCAN**: the LBD references the IAEA-recommended graded approach but the final perimeter has not been published. Tier A A8 currently rated `caution` pending CNCAN docketed decision.
