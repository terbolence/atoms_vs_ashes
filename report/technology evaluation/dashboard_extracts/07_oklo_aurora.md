# Oklo Aurora Powerhouse — Evidence Corpus

**Vendor:** Oklo Inc. (US, Santa Clara CA HQ; NYSE listed)
**Reference plant:** **Aurora Powerhouse**: factory-built integrated SMR, modular **15-75 MWe per module** (vendor advertises up to 100 MWe units in pipeline)
**Coolant / spectrum:** Liquid sodium (compact pool fast reactor) / **fast spectrum** — Generation IV
**Fuel:** **Metallic U-Zr alloy**, U-235 enrichment **≥10% to <20% (HALEU)**; first-core fuel from **EBR-II legacy stock at INL**
**HALEU dependency:** YES — first commercial-scale HALEU pipeline depends on **A3F (Advanced Fuel Fabrication Facility)** at INL — see `00_haleu_supply_snapshot.md`
**Lead first-of-a-kind project:** **Idaho National Laboratory (INL) Aurora-INL** — combined licence application
**Commercial offtake:** **Switch (data centres)** Master PPA signed Dec 2023 for up to **12 GWe** by 2044

## 1. NEA SMR Dashboard 3rd Edition extract

> **Reactor description:** Compact pool-type, integrated commercial fast reactor cooled with liquid sodium and using metallic uranium-zirconium fuel.
> Thermal power: 38 MWth — Core outlet temperature: 480 °C — Spectrum: fast — Fuel form: Metallic uranium-zirconium alloy — Base fissile material: U-235, enrichment ≥ 10% to < 20%.

(NEA Dashboard 3rd Ed (2025), p. 184.)

> **Licensing.** In 2020, Oklo submitted a Combined Licence Application (CLA) to the US Nuclear Regulatory Commission (NRC) to construct and operate the Aurora-INL Powerhouse, a small fast reactor with integrated heat and power generation, on a site to be located at the Idaho National Laboratory (INL). The licensing process was initiated in 2016. NRC denied the CLA in 2022, citing missing information on a number of safety topics. In 2024, Oklo submitted a Licensing Project Plan (LPP) for the Aurora-INL Powerhouse site to NRC, in which Oklo plans to submit a CLA in the second quarter of 2025 to support a 2027 deployment.

> **Siting.** In 2019, Oklo received from the US Department of Energy (DOE) a site use permit at the Idaho National Laboratory (INL), where Oklo plans to construct the Aurora-INL Powerhouse, the first commercial Aurora Powerhouse, by 2027. Oklo plans to deploy a fleet of additional Aurora Powerhouses across the United States and is conducting siting feasibility studies in California, Wyoming, Pennsylvania, and Tennessee. Oklo selected J.B. Henderson and Centerline Construction for site preparation and Aurora Powerhouse Constructability and Site Preparation Studies. In 2025, Oklo received approval from DOE to enter the design and engineering phase to deploy a commercial Aurora Powerhouse at INL by 2027/2028.

> **Financing.** Since 2014, Oklo has secured over USD 1.4 billion (USD 2023 1.5 billion) in private funding to support its development. In 2024, Oklo went public on the New York Stock Exchange. Earlier in 2017, Oklo had received a USD 2.6 million (USD 2023 3.4 million) DOE grant. Oklo has secured Power Purchase Agreements (PPAs) and Letters of Intent for over 14 GW with: Switch, Wyoming Hyperscale, Equinix, Diamondback Energy, Vertiv, Diamondback Energy, Korea Hydro & Nuclear Power, Centrus, RPower, Liberty Energy, and Prometheus Hyperscale. The PPA with Wyoming Hyperscale will support deployment of Aurora Powerhouses in Wyoming.

> **Supply chain.** Oklo has a Memorandum of Understanding with Westinghouse to advance the development of advanced reactors. Oklo has also signed Memoranda of Understanding (MoUs) with Atkins Realis to support advanced reactor projects in the United Kingdom and Aurora-INL Powerhouse construction. Oklo has signed an MoU with Korea Hydro and Nuclear Power for power, hydrogen, and steam generation. To accelerate development, Oklo plans to use 75% of components, parts, and assemblies from existing supply chains.

> **Engagement.** In 2024, Oklo's CEO has met multiple US elected officials. The CEO of NRC visited Oklo's headquarters in 2025. Oklo has launched the Oklo Insights platform, which informs the public on its operations as well as their environmental impact. The Aurora-INL Powerhouse project benefits from a positive Environment Compliance Permit, issued in 2020 from DOE. Oklo has also presented its activities to the public, including at the World Government Summit in Dubai, the Wyoming State Convention, and the European SMR Stakeholders Conference.

> **Fuel.** Oklo plans to use HALEU recycled from used fuel from the Experimental Breeder Reactor-II (EBR-II), which generated electricity for over 30 years and demonstrated the safety and economics of metal-fueled fast reactors at the Idaho National Laboratory. The recycled fuel will allow Oklo to demonstrate fuel recycling technology by recovering this material in collaboration with Argonne National Laboratory and Idaho National Laboratory under DOE-supported initiatives. In 2025, Oklo selected Eitter & Schaper Engineering to support the Front-end Engineering and Design (FEED) studies for its Advanced Fuel Fabrication Facility (A3F).

(NEA Dashboard 3rd Ed (2025), pp. 184-185.)

## 2. April 2026 evidence snapshot

### Aurora-INL CLA pathway

- **December 2024:** Oklo submitted a **Licensing Project Plan (LPP)** to NRC. The LPP, however, has been **publicly criticised by Oklo's CEO** as "unrealistic given improvements in regulatory environment" — Oklo plans to submit a CLA mid-2026 (revised from Q2 2025) for an INL FOAK Aurora.
- **March 2025:** Oklo received DOE approval to enter design and engineering phase at INL.
- **April 2025:** **NRC approved the safety evaluation report (SER)** for **Argonne National Laboratory's HALEU recycle process** that will produce Aurora's first-core fuel. ([NEI Magazine 2025-04](https://www.neimagazine.com/news/nrc-approves-argonne-fuel-process-for-oklo/))
- **April 2026:** **No CLA docketed yet** for Aurora-INL; vendor schedule is materially compressed and remains the largest schedule risk among the 9 designs.

### Aurora data-centre fleet

- **December 2023:** Switch master PPA for up to **12 GWe** by 2044.
- **Pipeline:** ≥14 GW total LOIs / PPAs (Switch, Wyoming Hyperscale, Equinix, Diamondback, Vertiv, KHNP, Centrus, RPower, Liberty Energy, Prometheus Hyperscale).
- **NYSE listing:** went public May 2024 (DA Davidson SPAC merger).

### Advanced Fuel Fabrication Facility (A3F)

- **Late 2025-2026:** FEED studies underway with Eitter & Schaper. Site is INL.
- **CP application:** Not yet docketed.
- **First commercial-scale HALEU output target:** post-2030.

## 4. Open data gaps

- **Romania / European deployment:** none publicly documented; the design is a US-only data-centre play in 2026.
- **Aurora is too small to substitute for VOYGR-6 / 462 MWe**: a 462 MWe Doicești profile would require ~30 × 15 MWe Aurora modules, or ~6 × 75 MWe modules. Per-module cost economics break down at this aggregation.
- **CAPEX disclosure:** Oklo has not published per-module overnight CAPEX. The model uses 6 500 USD/kWe FOAK at INL (vendor PPA economics suggest <USD 70/MWh LCOE if achieved); low/high 5 000 / 12 000 USD/kWe.
- **Fuel pathway:** First-core EBR-II legacy fuel is INL-only and **non-transferable**. Any non-INL deployment requires A3F and HALEU pipeline.
- **A2 Hard Gate:** **fail** for Romania COD-2033 — see `00_haleu_supply_snapshot.md`.
