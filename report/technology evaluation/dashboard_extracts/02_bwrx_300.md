# GE Vernova Hitachi Nuclear Energy BWRX-300 — Evidence Corpus

**Vendor:** GE Vernova Hitachi Nuclear Energy (GVH; joint venture renamed in 2024 from GE Hitachi Nuclear Energy)
**Reference plant:** **300 MWe net** boiling water reactor (single-module, single-unit per plant footprint, multi-unit per site)
**Coolant / spectrum:** Light water (BWR, natural circulation) / thermal
**Fuel:** UO₂ ceramic, U-235 enrichment < 5%, GNF Americas fuel assemblies (BWR-standard)
**Lead first-of-a-kind project:** OPG **Darlington New Nuclear Project** (Ontario, Canada) — 4 units; unit 1 first concrete 2025

## 1. NEA SMR Dashboard 3rd Edition extract

> **Reactor description:** Land-based boiling water reactor.
> Thermal power: 870 MWth — Core outlet temperature: 288 °C — Spectrum: thermal — Fuel form: Uranium-oxide ceramic — Base fissile material: U-235, enrichment < 5%.

(NEA Dashboard 3rd Ed (2025), p. 134.)

> **Licensing.** In 2024, the Canadian Nuclear Safety Commission (CNSC) confirmed the environmental assessment for BWRX-300 at Ontario Power Generation's (OPG) Darlington site. OPG's 2022 application for a licence to construct is under review by CSNC. In 2023, GE Vernova Hitachi (GVH) completed CNSC's vendor design review Phases 1-2. The US Nuclear Regulatory Commission (NRC) has approved five topical reports for BWRX-300; two more are under review. GVH has completed United Kingdom Generic Design Assessment Step 1 and started Step 2. In Poland, the National Atomic Energy Agency has issued a General Opinion that BWRX-300 is compliant with national nuclear safety requirements and the Ministry of Climate and Environment has issued decisions-in-principle for the construction of 24 BWRX-300s at six sites.

> **Siting.** In 2024, OPG completed early-phase site preparation for the first BWRX-300 at its Darlington site; site preparation for three additional units is underway. BWRX-300 has also been selected for potential deployment by: SaskPower in Canada; ORLEN Synthos Green Energy in Poland; and American Electric Power at the Rockport coal-fired plant in Indiana, United States. Tennessee Valley Authority (TVA) has partnered with GVH and announced it will work with Bechtel and Sargent & Lundy to evaluate potential deployment at the Clinch River site in the United States. BWRX-300 has been shortlisted by: Great British Nuclear in the United Kingdom; and Vattenfall for its Ringhals nuclear site in Sweden.

> **Financing.** GVH has secured CAD 970 million (USD 2023 746.6 million) from the Canadian Infrastructure Bank, and USD 7.2 million (USD 2023 8.5 million) from the US Department of Energy (DOE) Advanced Research Projects Agency-Energy. In 2021, the Province of Ontario enabled OPG to recover project costs from the electricity ratepayer through a regulatory amendment. In 2023, GVH, TVA, OPG and Synthos Green Energy (SGE) committed to invest USD 400 million. In 2024, GVH received a GBP 33.6 million (USD 2023 40.7 million) grant from the UK Future Nuclear Enabling Fund for development in the United Kingdom. In 2025, TVA announced that it will lead, along with a coalition of partners, an application for up to USD 800 million from the US DOE's Generation III+ Small Modular Reactor Program to support BWRX-300 in the United States.

> **Supply chain.** In Canada, GVH is partnering with OPG, Aecon and AtkinsRéalis, and has selected: E.S. Fox for site preparation; BWXT for reactor pressure vessel; Velan for industrial valves; and Worley Chemetics for the isolation condenser system. In the United Kingdom, GVH has signed Memorandums of Understanding (MoUs) with suppliers, including Aecon UK, AtkinsRéalis UK, Amentum Clean Energy (formerly Jacobs Clean Energy), Laing O'Rourke, Boccard UK, Cavendish Nuclear, and Sheffield Forgemasters, and has contracted Instance BV to provide input on the design. In 2024, GVH formed a global supplier's group with BWXT Canada being the first to join, followed by Worley Chemetics. BWRX-300 was also selected for a Project-Working Group under the European Industrial Alliance on SMRs.

> **Engagement.** In Canada, the Darlington project has been endorsed by both provincial and federal governments. OPG engages the public through information sessions, workshops, hearings, and community events. GVH also has an agreement in place with the Saskatchewan Industrial and Mining Suppliers Association to engage with local suppliers to advance the potential deployment in Saskatchewan. Internationally, GVH has established a series of MoUs including with Kärnfull Next in Sweden and with Emirates Nuclear Energy Corporation in the United Arab Emirates for the potential deployment of BWRX-300. In 2025, Fermi Energia submitted an application to Estonia's Ministry of Economic Affairs and Communications to initiate a state planning process for a nuclear power plant based on BWRX-300 technology.

> **Fuel.** The BWRX-300 reactor uses fuel that is proven, commercially available and currently being used in operating boiling water reactors. OPG has selected suppliers for the first fuel contracts for its Darlington project in Canada, where up to four BWRX-300 SMRs are planned. These suppliers include: Cameco for natural uranium hexafluoride; Urenco US for uranium enrichment services; Orano for additional enriched uranium product; and Global Nuclear Fuel-Americas, a joint venture led by GVH, for fuel fabrication and fuel assemblies. In 2025, GE Vernova, the parent company of GVH, announced plans to invest more than USD 50 million (USD 2023 47.1 million) in GVH's Wilmington site in North Carolina to expand fuel manufacturing capacity for boiling water reactors, including the BWRX-300.

(NEA Dashboard 3rd Ed (2025), pp. 134-135.)

## 2. April 2026 evidence snapshot

### Darlington (Canada)

- **April 2025:** CNSC issued the **construction licence** for the first BWRX-300 unit at Darlington. ([CNSC project page](https://www.cnsc-ccsn.gc.ca/eng/reactors/new-reactor-power-plant-projects/new-reactor-power-plant-facilities/darlington-new-nuclear-project/))
- **May 2025:** Construction began at Darlington. ([GE Vernova product page](https://www.gevernova.com/nuclear/carbon-free-power/bwrx-300-small-modular-reactor))
- **March 2026:** OPG **completed shaft excavation and dewatering works** for unit 1; the first regulatory hold point (reactor building foundation) was lifted on **30 March 2026**. ([CNSC project page](https://www.cnsc-ccsn.gc.ca/eng/reactors/new-reactor-power-plant-projects/new-reactor-power-plant-facilities/darlington-new-nuclear-project/); [NucNet 2026-01](https://www.nucnet.org/news/opg-completes-excavation-works-at-darlington-bwrx-300-smr-project-3-1-2026))
- **March 2026:** OPG **submitted an operating-licence application** for unit 1. ([NucNet 2026-04-05](https://www.nucnet.org/news/opg-submits-application-for-licence-to-operate-first-darlington-smr-4-5-2026))
- **Commercial operation target:** end-2030 / late 2029-2030 per GE Vernova. ([GE Vernova product page](https://www.gevernova.com/nuclear/carbon-free-power/bwrx-300-small-modular-reactor))
- **Total Darlington project cost (4 units):** **CAD 20.9 billion** (~USD 15.1 billion); first unit CAD 6.1 B + CAD 1.6 B shared infrastructure = CAD 7.7 B (~USD 5.6 B) → **~USD 25 000 / kWe FOAK**; 4th unit estimated CAD 4.1 B (~USD 2.9 B) → ~USD 9 700 / kWe — implies a learning curve with **~33 % cost reduction** between unit 1 and unit 4. ([WNN 2025-05](https://www.world-nuclear-news.org/articles/what-is-the-budget-for-canadas-first-smr-project); [WNR 2025-05](https://www.worldnuclearreport.org/Ontario-s-Darlington-SMR-project-to-cost-nearly-21-billion-significantly-higher))

### TVA Clinch River (USA)

- **April–May 2025:** TVA submitted **Construction Permit Application (CPA)** for the first BWRX-300 unit at the **Clinch River Nuclear Site**, Tennessee. ([Federal Register 2025-06-24](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2025/06/24/2025-11036/tennessee-valley-authority-clinch-river-nuclear-site-construction-permit-application))
- **July 2025:** NRC **docketed** the application for an **18-month review**. ([DOE 2025-07](https://www.energy.gov/ne/articles/nrc-dockets-construction-permit-application-tva-small-modular-reactor))
- NRC schedule: **Final Safety Evaluation Report (FSER) targeted Nov 2026**; Final Environmental Impact Statement (FEIS) / FONSI targeted June 2026. ([NRC Clinch River project page](https://www.nrc.gov/reactors/new-reactors/advanced/who-were-working-with/applicant-projects/clinch-river))

### Sweden / Europe

- **Vattenfall** has **down-selected BWRX-300 as a finalist** for new nuclear at the **Ringhals** site (alongside Rolls-Royce SMR). ([GE Vernova product page](https://www.gevernova.com/nuclear/carbon-free-power/bwrx-300-small-modular-reactor))
- **April 2026:** Sweden's **Afry deployment agreement** announced for engineering support. ([NucNet 2026-04-05](https://www.nucnet.org/news/opg-submits-application-for-licence-to-operate-first-darlington-smr-4-5-2026))
- **Estonia (Fermi Energia):** state planning application submitted in 2025; first BWRX-300 deployment 2030s.
- **Poland (Orlen Synthos Green Energy):** Decision-in-Principle for **24 BWRX-300 units at 6 sites** (NEA Dashboard p. 134); environmental and siting work underway.
- **UK GDA:** Step 1 complete; **Step 2** in progress as of 2025-2026.

## 4. Open data gaps

- **Romania interest in BWRX-300:** none publicly documented; SNN is contractually committed to NuScale at Doicești. For the comparison this design is a hypothetical alternative for a second Romanian site.
- **NOAK pricing**: even the Darlington learning curve (CAD 6.1 B → 4.1 B) gives only one data point; long-run NOAK ($/kWe) is bracketed by the model with a 6–12 % learning rate.
- **First fuel delivery for Clinch River**: GNF-A Wilmington expansion in 2025 is a precondition.
