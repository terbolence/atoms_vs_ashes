# Kairos Power KP-FHR (Hermes 1, Hermes 2, KP-FHR-140) — Evidence Corpus

**Vendor:** Kairos Power LLC (US, Albuquerque NM HQ)
**Reactor family:**
- **Hermes 1** — 35 MWth low-power demonstration reactor (non-power-producing); construction permit Dec 2023 (NRC).
- **Hermes 2** — first commercial-scale demo, **50 MWe** electrical (per design release; pebble-bed FHR); CP issued Nov 2024; ground broken **17 April 2026**.
- **KP-FHR-140** — commercial reference plant, **140 MWe** (2×35 MWth modules); separate NRC commercial licensing pathway not yet docketed.
**Coolant / spectrum:** **High-temperature fluoride salt (FLiBe)** / thermal — Generation IV
**Fuel:** **TRISO pebbles**, U-235 enrichment **≥10% to <20% (HALEU)**
**HALEU dependency:** YES — see `00_haleu_supply_snapshot.md` for A2 implications
**Lead site:** Oak Ridge, Tennessee (East Tennessee Technology Park campus)
**Major commercial offtake:** **Google** Master Plant Development Agreement (Oct 2024) for **500 MW by 2035**

## 1. NEA SMR Dashboard 3rd Edition extract

> **Reactor description:** High temperature fluoride salt-cooled demonstration reactor using TRISO pebble fuel.
> Thermal power: 35 MWth — Core outlet temperature: 650 °C — Spectrum: thermal — Fuel form: Uranium-oxide ceramic TRISO pebble — Base fissile material: U-235, enrichment ≥ 10% to < 20%.

(NEA Dashboard 3rd Ed (2025), p. 166.)

> **Licensing.** Hermes is a 35 MWth, non-power demonstration reactor planned on the East Tennessee Technology Park campus in Oak Ridge, Tennessee (United States), supporting the development of Kairos Power's 320 MWth (75 MWe) commercial KP-FHR technology. Kairos Power submitted a Construction Permit Application (CPA) to the US Nuclear Regulatory Commission (NRC) under a regulatory pathway distinct from commercial reactors' licensing process. In December 2023, the NRC issued a Construction Permit to Kairos Power for the Hermes reactor.

> **Siting.** Kairos Power is working to deploy the Hermes test reactor on 30 acres of a larger plot purchased on the East Tennessee Technology Park campus in Oak Ridge, which was previously reclaimed from the US Department of Energy (DOE). In December 2023, the NRC issued a Construction Permit to Kairos Power to build the one-of-a-kind Hermes reactor on this site. Kairos Power has engaged Barnard Construction Company, a heavy-civil construction firm, to carry out site work and excavation at the Hermes site in Oak Ridge, with work commencing in July 2024.

> **Financing.** In 2024, Kairos Power signed a power purchase agreement with Google for up to 500 MWe. Also in 2024, Kairos Power and the US Department of Energy (DOE) signed a Technology Investment Agreement to support the design, construction, and operation of the Hermes demonstration reactor. This agreement includes milestones and performance-based criteria to facilitate the payment of a USD 303 million (USD 2023 356.7 million) grant that Kairos Power was awarded in 2020 under the US Advanced Reactor Demonstration Program. Kairos Power is also providing private investments supporting the project and its associated infrastructure. Kairos Power was selected to receive over USD 10 million (USD 2023 12 million) in matching grants from various DOE initiatives in 2018-2019 to support the design and licensing of the KP-FHR.

> **Supply chain.** Barnard Construction started work on the Hermes Oak Ridge site in 2024. Kairos Power is enhancing its in-house capabilities on its Albuquerque campus, focusing on system design, manufacturing, testing, salt production, and TRISO fuel and pebble development. Kairos Power is manufacturing non-nuclear Engineering Test Units (ETUs). The ETU 2.0 reactor vessel was completed in 2025. Kairos Power received ASME certification for U-stamped vessels in 2022 and began constructing a Salt Production Facility in 2024. A collaboration between Materion and Kairos Power produced 14 tons of salts by late 2023. The company has received multiple DOE GAIN vouchers. In 2022, Kairos Power launched its Operations, Manufacturing, and Development Alliance (KP-OMADA), an advisory group of North American utilities.

> **Engagement.** Kairos Power launched a virtual meeting space and partnered with the American Museum of Science and Energy to engage local communities. Kairos Power projects have been endorsed by government officials, including New Mexico Governor Michelle Grisham, Senator Martin Heinrich, Albuquerque Mayor Tim Keller, Tennessee Lieutenant Governor Randy McNally, and Oak Ridge Mayor Warren Gooch. Kairos Power has sponsored initiatives like Nuclear Science Week and Discover STEAM Week and participated in the UC Berkeley Clean Energy Speaker Series. In 2024, Texas A&M University System Chancellor John Sharp announced the university's potential interest in hosting SMRs from Kairos Power and other companies. Kairos Power received the BloombergNEF Pioneer Award in 2022 for advancements in nuclear technology.

> **Fuel.** In 2023, Kairos Power and Urenco US signed a Memorandum of Understanding to explore future collaboration areas towards securing HALEU supply for Kairos KP-FHR commercial reactors. The Hermes test reactor is designed to use TRISO fuel pebbles, which will be produced at the New Mexico lab's Low Enriched Fuel Fabrication Facility (LEFFF) under an agreement between Los Alamos National Laboratory and Kairos Power. The LEFFF will host several fuel manufacturing processes being developed by Kairos Power at its TRISO and Pebble Development Labs in Albuquerque, New Mexico.

(NEA Dashboard 3rd Ed (2025), pp. 166-167.)

## 2. April 2026 evidence snapshot

### Hermes 1 (35 MWth low-power demo)

- **December 2023:** NRC issued **Construction Permit** for Hermes — the first non-water-cooled reactor to receive an NRC CP. ([Kairos Tennessee location page](https://www.kairospower.com/locations/tennessee))
- **May 2025:** Construction of Hermes Demonstration Reactor began at Oak Ridge.
- **April 2026:** Foundation work in progress (drilled piers, seismic isolators).

### Hermes 2 (50 MWe commercial-scale demo)

- **November 2024:** NRC issued Construction Permit for Hermes 2 — **first power-producing Generation IV reactor** to receive an NRC CP. ([Kairos press release 2026-04-17](https://www.kairospower.com/updates/kairos-power-breaks-ground-on-hermes-2-demonstration-plant))
- **17 April 2026:** Kairos Power **broke ground** on Hermes 2. ([Kairos press release](https://www.kairospower.com/updates/kairos-power-breaks-ground-on-hermes-2-demonstration-plant); [WNN 2026-04-20](https://world-nuclear-news.org/articles/kairos-breaks-ground-for-hermes-2-reactor); [BriefGlance](https://briefglance.com/articles/kairos-power-breaks-ground-on-gen-iv-reactor-backed-by-google))
- **August 2025:** Kairos Power and **TVA signed PPA** for up to **50 MW** electricity from Hermes 2 to TVA grid; clean-energy credits flow to Google's data centres in TN/AL. ([10 Big Wins 2025 LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/10-big-wins-kairos-power-2025-kairospower-6jmke); [OakRidger 2026-03-11](https://www.oakridger.com/story/news/local/2026/03/11/touring-oak-ridges-future-nuclear-energy-hub-tva-and-kairos-power/89055479007/))
- **OakRidger schedule:** Hermes 2 commercial operation **scheduled for 2030**.

### Google Master Plant Development Agreement

- **October 2024:** Master Plant Development Agreement signed; Kairos Power and Google partner to deploy a fleet of advanced reactors **totalling 500 MW by 2035**. ([Kairos press release 2024-10-14](https://www.kairospower.com/updates/google-and-kairos-power-partner-to-deploy-500-mw-of-clean-electricity-generation); [Google blog](https://blog.google/company-news/outreach-and-initiatives/sustainability/google-kairos-power-nuclear-energy-agreement/))
- First deployment by 2030 (Hermes 2); subsequent units 2030-2035.

### Commercial KP-FHR-140

- The full commercial reference plant is **2 × 35 MWth ≈ 70 MWe per module, 140 MWe per dual-module plant** (NEA cites 75 MWe per "320 MWth commercial KP-FHR"; vendor public materials and Hermes 2 sizing extrapolate to 50 MWe single-module commercial / 140 MWe twin).
- Commercial KP-FHR has **not yet docketed an NRC commercial CP application**; the Hermes 1/2 use a research-reactor-style pathway (10 CFR 50.21(c) class).

### Fuel supply (HALEU pebbles)

- **LEFFF (Los Alamos)** for Hermes initial cores.
- **Urenco MoU** for KP-FHR commercial HALEU supply (2023, non-binding).
- **Salt Production Facility** under construction at Albuquerque since 2024; 14 t FLiBe produced by late 2023 with Materion.

## 4. Open data gaps

- **Romania / European deployment:** none publicly documented. KP-FHR-140 commercial CP application is required before any non-US deployment is realistic, and this is not yet docketed.
- **Commercial CAPEX disclosure:** Kairos has not published commercial KP-FHR-140 CAPEX. The model uses 8 500 USD/kWe FOAK (highly speculative), low/high 6 500 / 12 000 USD/kWe.
- **HALEU pipeline for commercial fleet:** Urenco MoU is non-binding; depends on Centrus/General Matter/Urenco capacity expansion (see `00_haleu_supply_snapshot.md`).
- **Size mismatch with Doicești-style 462 MWe coal-replacement profile:** Hermes 2 is 50 MWe; KP-FHR-140 is 140 MWe. To match the 462 MWe Doicești scope you would need ~3.3 KP-FHR-140 plants, increasing siting and licensing cost.
