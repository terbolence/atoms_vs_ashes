# TerraPower Natrium — Evidence Corpus

**Vendor:** TerraPower LLC (US, Bellevue WA HQ; backed by Bill Gates / Cascade Investment)
**Reference plant:** **345 MWe nominal continuous output, with energy-storage system enabling 500 MWe peak** for ~5.5 hours
**Coolant / spectrum:** Liquid sodium (pool-type) / **fast spectrum** — Generation IV
**Energy storage:** Integrated **Gigawatt-Hour Energy Storage System (molten salt)** — boosts dispatchable peaking capacity
**Fuel:** **Metallic U-Zr alloy**, U-235 enrichment **≥10% to <20% (HALEU)**
**HALEU dependency:** YES — see `00_haleu_supply_snapshot.md`. First-core HALEU is the critical-path schedule item.
**Lead first-of-a-kind project:** **Kemmerer-1**, Wyoming (former Naughton coal plant site)
**Major commercial offtake:** PacifiCorp (Berkshire Hathaway Energy) — operator of the FOAK Kemmerer-1

## 1. NEA SMR Dashboard 3rd Edition extract

> **Reactor description:** Sodium fast reactor with integrated energy storage based on molten salt.
> Thermal power: 840 MWth — Core outlet temperature: 510 °C — Spectrum: fast — Fuel form: Metallic uranium-zirconium alloy — Base fissile material: U-235, enrichment ≥ 10% to < 20%.

(NEA Dashboard 3rd Ed (2025), p. 226.)

> **Licensing.** In 2024, the US Nuclear Regulatory Commission (NRC) approved TerraPower's Construction Permit Application (CPA) for the Natrium reactor demonstration project. Public hearings on the CPA, a critical step before NRC issuance, are scheduled to be held in late 2025. Even before this approval, TerraPower started non-nuclear construction at the Wyoming site in 2024. TerraPower also started pre-licensing engagements with the Korea Hydro & Nuclear Power. In 2024, TerraPower also signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) with the United Arab Emirates Federal Authority for Nuclear Regulation (FANR), allowing them to start the regulatory authorisation process.

> **Siting.** In June 2024, TerraPower started excavation work at the Natrium FOAK site in Kemmerer, Wyoming. In 2024, TerraPower formed a partnership with Sabey Data Centers and the State of Wyoming to support the deployment of an additional Natrium reactor in Wyoming. Also in 2024, TerraPower signed an MoU with Energy Northwest to advance feasibility studies for the deployment of multiple Natrium reactors. PacifiCorp, an electric utility, has expressed plans to potentially deploy multiple additional Natrium plants. TerraPower signed an MoU with Korea Hydro & Nuclear Power on the demonstration and deployment of Natrium SMRs and other small modular reactors.

> **Financing.** TerraPower secured matching funds in 2020 of USD 2 billion (USD 2023 2.4 billion) from the US Department of Energy for the construction and operation of the Natrium FOAK at the Kemmerer Power Station Unit 1 (KPS-1) site. In 2022, TerraPower secured an additional USD 750 million (USD 2023 798 million) of investment for the project, including from Bill Gates and SK Inc. In 2024, TerraPower secured nearly USD 9 million (USD 2023 8.7 million) from the US Defense Advanced Research Projects Agency. In addition to the Wyoming Energy Authority providing USD 7 million (USD 2023 6.8 million) in 2024, the State of Wyoming has invested USD 5 million (USD 2023 5.5 million) in matching funds.

> **Supply chain.** Members of TerraPower's supply chain include: Bechtel for engineering, procurement, design and construction; Doosan Enerbility for the reactor pressure vessel and major reactor components; Centrus Energy for the development of HALEU production capabilities; Westinghouse to design and supply heat exchangers; Doosan Enerbility for the reactor vessel; AECOM for design and engineering services for digital and instrumentation and control systems; and HD Hyundai Heavy Industries for forging components. In 2025, TerraPower has signed agreements with: AtkinsRéalis to advance the deployment of the Natrium reactor across multiple regions in the United States; PaR Systems to manufacture and deliver the in-vessel handling machine; and Curtiss-Wright to design and supply the protection system.

> **Engagement.** TerraPower has engaged with multiple stakeholders, including residents of Kemmerer, Wyoming, where TerraPower built a community office and recently funded a youth coding camp at Western Wyoming Community College. The Natrium project has been endorsed by Wyoming Governor Mark Gordon. TerraPower has presented its plans to deploy the Natrium reactor at multiple events, including at the Wyoming Energy Forum, Sustainable Earth Symposium, and the Energy Communities Alliance Annual Conference.

> **Fuel.** The Natrium reactor uses a metallic uranium-zirconium HALEU fuel that has gone through extensive operational research at the Idaho National Laboratory's Experimental Breeder Reactor-II (EBR-II), which operated for over 30 years. To support a long-term fuel supply, TerraPower has signed an MoU with Centrus Energy on the development of HALEU production capabilities for Natrium. In 2024, TerraPower received a USD 9 million (USD 2023 8.7 million) cooperative agreement to design fuel forms for fast-spectrum advanced reactors. In 2025, TerraPower and Framatome inaugurated the Natrium fuel pilot manufacturing facility in Romans-sur-Isère, France, allowing the production of the first metallic fuel test pins.

(NEA Dashboard 3rd Ed (2025), pp. 226-227.)

## 2. April 2026 evidence snapshot

### NRC Construction Permit issuance

- **18 December 2025:** NRC **issued the Construction Permit** for the Kemmerer Unit 1 Natrium reactor. ([NRC project page](https://www.nrc.gov/reactors/non-power/new-fac/kemmerer.html); [NRC release 26-028](https://www.nrc.gov/sites/default/files/cdn/doc-collection-news/2026/26-028.pdf); [WNN 2025-12-18](https://www.world-nuclear-news.org/articles/nrc-issues-natrium-construction-permit); [TerraPower press 2025-12-18](https://www.terrapower.com/wyoming-natrium-construction-permit/))
- This is the **first sodium-cooled fast reactor CP issued by NRC for commercial-scale deployment**.
- Per the NRC: NRC will continue review of the **operating licence application** (separate filing); operations would not begin before licence issuance.

### Wyoming construction status

- **Mid-2024:** Non-nuclear construction began (excavation, site preparation).
- **December 2025:** Sodium test facility (UK Sheffield) ready for components.
- **Schedule risk:** Vendor publicly **slipped first power from 2030 to 2031–2032** in mid-2024 due to HALEU fuel uncertainty. ([WNA Natrium](https://world-nuclear.org/information-library/facts-and-figures/reactor-database/terrapower-natrium))

### Romans-sur-Isère pilot fuel facility (Framatome / France)

- **2025:** TerraPower and Framatome **inaugurated the Natrium fuel pilot manufacturing facility** in Romans-sur-Isère, France. Allows production of the first metallic fuel test pins.
- This is the only European industrial footprint Natrium currently has, but it is fuel-pilot scale, not commercial reactor deployment.

### HALEU and Centrus

- TerraPower has the **MoU with Centrus** for HALEU. Centrus produced ~1 t HALEU under the DOE programme by 2025 — sufficient for early test pins, **not for a 9-tonne first core**.
- Russian HALEU import ban (total 2028) closes the historical Russian-origin pathway.

### Other 2025-2026 milestones

- **2025:** TerraPower signed agreements with AtkinsRéalis, PaR Systems, Curtiss-Wright as supply chain expansions.
- **Korea:** KHNP MoU active; FANR (UAE) MoU active.

## 4. Open data gaps

- **Romania / European deployment:** none publicly documented for a Natrium plant. Romans-sur-Isère pilot is fuel-only.
- **HALEU first-core delivery date:** the binding constraint. Even with NRC CP in hand, Natrium cannot start operations without a 9-tonne HALEU initial core, which Centrus cannot deliver before 2030 at current production capacity.
- **CAPEX disclosure:** Natrium reported **USD 4 billion total project cost** in 2024 (up from USD 1 B initial estimate); for 345 MWe nominal that is ~11 600 USD/kWe — but ESS storage capacity makes a direct $/kWe comparison unfair. The model uses 11 000 USD/kWe FOAK on the 345 MWe baseload basis, low/high 8 500 / 14 500.
- **Storage value monetization:** Natrium's 500 MWe peak / 5.5 h ESS only realises value in markets with intra-day price spreads — Romania's DAM has limited spread for now.
