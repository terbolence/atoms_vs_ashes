# Holtec International SMR-300 — Evidence Corpus

**Vendor:** Holtec International (US-headquartered; UK and Ukraine subsidiaries active)
**Reference plant:** **300 MWe net** integral pressurised water reactor (1 050 MWth)
**Coolant / spectrum:** Light water (PWR) / thermal
**Fuel:** UO₂ ceramic, U-235 enrichment < 5%, Framatome **GAIA 17×17** fuel assembly (existing PWR-standard)
**Lead first-of-a-kind project:** **Palisades Energy Center, Michigan** — Pioneer Units 1 & 2 alongside the restarted PWR
**CEE relevance:** Ukraine MoUs (Energoatom); UK GDA in progress (Cottam, Nottinghamshire siting work)

## 1. NEA SMR Dashboard 3rd Edition extract

> **Reactor description:** Land-based pressurised light water reactor.
> Thermal power: 1 050 MWth — Core outlet temperature: 321 °C — Spectrum: thermal — Fuel form: Uranium-oxide ceramic — Base fissile material: U-235, enrichment < 5%.

(NEA Dashboard 3rd Ed (2025), p. 144.)

> **Licensing.** In 2023, Holtec International (Holtec) renamed its "SMR-160" to "SMR-300" with a power increase from 525 to 1 050 MWth. The SMR-300 is expected to benefit from pre-licensing activities for the SMR-160. Prior to this change, Holtec had completed Phase 1 of the pre-licensing vendor design review with the Canadian Nuclear Safety Commission (CNSC) in 2020. It continues pre-licensing activities with the US Nuclear Regulatory Commission (NRC) with its new design. This includes meetings with the NRC regarding deployment at its Palisade site for which it plans to submit a construction permit application in 2026. In 2024, Holtec completed step 1 of UK Office for Nuclear Regulation's (ONR) Generic Design Assessment (GDA). In 2024, Holtec also started GDA step 2, which it expects to complete in 2025.

> **Siting.** In the United States, Holtec plans two SMR-300 units at the Palisades site it acquired in Michigan in 2022. Holtec has signed an agreement with Enercon to support the preparation of their application to the NRC for deployment of the two SMR-300 at the Palisades site. Holtec is also considering deploying an SMR at its Oyster Creek nuclear site in New Jersey. Holtec has a Memorandum of Agreement to assess SMR deployment feasibility at Entergy Corporation sites. Holtec also has two co-operation agreements with NAEK Energoatom to plan for the deployment of SMRs in Ukraine. SMR-300 was shortlisted with three other SMRs by Great British Nuclear (GBN) for the next phase of the innovative nuclear technology competition for potential deployment in the United Kingdom.

> **Financing.** In 2020, Holtec secured USD 116 million (USD 2023 136.5 million) over seven years from the US Department of Energy (DOE) Advanced Reactor Demonstration Program for early-stage design, engineering and licensing. In 2021, Holtec stated it had invested USD 400 million (USD 2023 449.7 million) in its SMR programme. Holtec is a member of the Mid-Atlantic Clean Hydrogen Hub selected by DOE to receive up to USD 750 million. In 2023, the Korea Trade Insurance Corporation and the Export-Import Bank of Korea signed agreements with Holtec and Hyundai Engineering & Construction to support financing of Holtec SMR projects. In 2024, the UK Department for Energy Security and Net Zero (DESNZ) awarded GPB 30 million (USD 2023 36.4 million) to Holtec to support the GDA pre-licensing process for the SMR-300.

> **Supply chain.** Holtec has in-house nuclear engineering and construction capabilities. Holtec has contracted Mitsubishi Electric, and Hyundai Engineering and Construction (Hyundai E&C). In 2022, DOE approved part 1 of a loan application for Holtec to expand manufacturing in the United States. Starting in 2018 and renewed in 2024, Holtec has been working with NAEK Energoatom on a manufacturing hub in Ukraine. In 2024: Holtec selected South Yorkshire, United Kingdom, for its SMR factory, a GPB 1.5 billion (USD 2023 1.9 billion) investment pending final decision; Holtec and Hyundai E&C signed partnership agreements with Balfour Beatty and Mott McDonald for the UK GBN competition; and Holtec signed Memoranda of Understanding with Sheffield Forgemaster and Sheffield Advanced Manufacturing Research Centre.

> **Engagement.** In 2022, Holtec presented its SMR activities to the Van Buren County Council, Michigan, United States. In 2023, Holtec participated in the Southwest Public Policy Institute's podcast dedicated to SMRs. Holtec senior staff have also participated in various other podcasts and TV interviews to discuss SMR plans. In 2024, Holtec CEO met with India's Prime Minister in New York and discussed possible cooperation on nuclear energy. In 2024, South Yorkshire Mayor Oliver Coppard publicly welcomed plans to manufacture SMRs in South Yorkshire, United Kingdom.

> **Fuel.** The SMR-300 uses LEU fuel that is the current industry standard for water-cooled reactor technologies. In 2018, Holtec International signed an MoU with Global Nuclear Fuel-Americas, a joint venture led by GE Hitachi Nuclear Energy, to develop nuclear fuel and control rod drive mechanisms for Holtec's SMR. In 2020, Holtec signed a contract with Framatome, a French nuclear fuel manufacturer, to supply nuclear fuel the SMR-300 in the form of the Framatome's GAIA 17x17 fuel assembly that is already used in operating pressurised light-water reactors.

(NEA Dashboard 3rd Ed (2025), pp. 144-145.)

## 2. April 2026 evidence snapshot

### Palisades / Pioneer Units 1 & 2 (USA)

- **31 December 2025:** Holtec subsidiary "SMR, LLC" submitted **Part 1 of a phased Construction Permit Application (CPA)** to the NRC, including a **Limited Work Authorization (LWA)** request for Pioneer Units 1 and 2 at Palisades. ([ANS 2026-01-14](https://www.ans.org/news/2026-01-14/article-7673/holtec-submits-partial-construction-permit-application-for-smrs-at-palisades/); [Federal Register 2026-01-22](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/01/22/2026-01143/smr-llc-pioneer-units-1-and-2-phased-construction-permit-application-limited-work-authorization))
- **13 February 2026:** NRC **accepted the application for review**; Federal Register notice published 27 Feb 2026. ([NRC project page](https://www.nrc.gov/reactors/new-reactors/advanced/who-were-working-with/pre-application-activities/holtec/smr300); [NucNet 2026-03-02](https://www.nucnet.org/news/us-nuclear-regulator-accepts-partial-construction-permit-application-for-palisades-smrs-2-3-2026))
- **Part 2 of CPA**: expected mid-2027 per Holtec planning.
- **Holtec stated COD target:** **2030** for the first SMR-300 unit. ([Energy Communities Alliance Holtec slides](https://energy-communities-alliance.squarespace.com/s/Holtec-Slides.pdf))
- **DOE funding:** USD 400 million awarded in 2025-2026 to support SMR-300 development.

### UK GDA & Cottam siting

- **31 March 2026:** SMR-300 **completed Step 2 (Fundamental Assessment) of the UK GDA**; ONR, Environment Agency, and Natural Resources Wales found "no fundamental shortfalls" in the design across safety, security, safeguards, environment. ([ONR 2026-03](https://www.onr.org.uk/news/all-news/2026/03/holtec-s-smr-300-completes-generic-design-assessment); [WNN 2026-03](https://www.world-nuclear-news.org/articles/holtec-smr-design-clears-key-uk-regulatory-stage); [GOV.UK Step 2 closure](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/gda-step-2-of-the-holtec-smr-300-fundamental-assessment/gda-step-2-of-the-holtec-international-smr-300-fundamental-assessment-public-summary))
- 14 regulatory observations carried into Step 3 (engaged positively).

### Manufacturing footprint (UK)

- South Yorkshire (Sheffield) preferred for the **Holtec Heavy Industries (HHI)** SMR factory; GBP 1.5 B investment **pending final decision** (NEA Dashboard p. 144). No formal start-construction announcement as of April 2026.

### Ukraine

- 2018-2024 active MoU and co-operation agreements with **Energoatom** for both deployment and a manufacturing hub. War-related deployment timing remains highly uncertain.

## 4. Open data gaps

- **Romania:** no SMR-300 siting interest publicly documented.
- **CAPEX disclosure:** Holtec has not published a detailed overnight CAPEX. The model uses 5 800 USD/kWe FOAK at Palisades (consistent with company guidance to investors of "competitive with combined-cycle gas including ETS"); low/high 4 800 / 7 500 USD/kWe.
- **Co-located restart leverage:** the SMR-300 sites alongside the restarted Palisades PWR; cost share with restart cannot be cleanly disaggregated from public data.
- **Cottam (Nottinghamshire UK) siting confirmation:** referenced in GOV.UK GDA Step 2 page but no land-rights agreement disclosed.
