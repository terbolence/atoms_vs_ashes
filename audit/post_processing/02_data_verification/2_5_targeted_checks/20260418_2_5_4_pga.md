### Interpretation

- **Doicești and Feldioara** have the highest PGA values (0.24–0.24 g), consistent with their location in the southern Carpathian foreland where Vrancea attenuation is moderate.
- **Brăila** is east of Vrancea and shows a slightly lower PGA (0.22 g), consistent with attenuation through the Wallachian/Moldavian platform.
- **Rovinari and Turceni** are in Oltenia (western Romania), far from Vrancea, with PGA₄₇₅ below 0.10 g — consistent with the rapid attenuation westward.
- The PGA₂₄₇₅/PGA₄₇₅ ratio ranges from 1.69 (Brăila) to 1.95 (Turceni), typical for the ESHM13 hazard curves in stable cratonic regions.

## 6. Hazard curve completeness

All 5 anchor sites have a full 25-point hazard curve stored in `spectral_accel_json`. The UHS (Uniform Hazard Spectrum) endpoint was not functional at query time (`uhs: None`), so spectral acceleration at different periods is unavailable. This means:

- PGA is available and correct.
- SA(T) at structural periods (0.1 s, 0.2 s, 0.5 s, 1.0 s, 2.0 s) is **not** available.
- For nuclear siting, SA at 0.1 s and 1.0 s are typically required alongside PGA for PSHA-level analysis.
