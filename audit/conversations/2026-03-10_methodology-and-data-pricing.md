# Methodology Discussion and Data Source Pricing

**Date:** 2026-03-10 to 2026-03-11
**Session ID:** 8f4e09d3-e5dd-4e4e-9c6c-e8f21904f512

## Objective

Multiple topics addressed in a single extended session:
1. Clarify the screening methodology (survey → screening → ranking) against IAEA SSG-35 and EPRI frameworks.
2. Assess whether coal-site suitability implies nuclear-site acceptability.
3. Identify which data sources in `requirements/07_data_requirements.md` are difficult to obtain or require payment.
4. Add pricing/cost information to the data requirements and create a data budget.

## Key Decisions

- Confirmed the 3-phase siting methodology (survey → exclusionary + discretionary screening → ranking) is consistent with both SSG-35 and EPRI. The two frameworks can be reconciled and used together.
- Identified that coal-site suitability does not guarantee nuclear acceptability — seismic, flood, and population criteria are significantly stricter for nuclear.
- Catalogued data sources by accessibility: most are free/public APIs; a few (EPRI guide, some national geological surveys, Copernicus CDS for large datasets) may have access costs.
- Created a data budget document with per-source cost estimates.
- Updated `requirements/07_data_requirements.md` with pricing information.
