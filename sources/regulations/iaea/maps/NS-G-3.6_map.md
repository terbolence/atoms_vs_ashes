# Document Map: IAEA NS-G-3.6
**Title:** Geotechnical Aspects of Site Evaluation and Foundations for Nuclear Power Plants  
**Series:** IAEA Safety Standards Series No. NS-G-3.6 (Safety Guide)  
**Year:** 2004 | **Pages:** 67 | **Superseded by:** SSG-93

---

## Purpose
Provides recommendations on geotechnical site investigation, site characterization, and foundation evaluation for nuclear power plants. Addresses subsurface conditions, soil behaviour, slope stability, liquefaction, and foundation design. Referenced by SSG-35 for geotechnical screening criteria.

---

## Section Map

### Section 1: Introduction (pp. 1–3, §1.1–1.9)
- Scope: Geotechnical investigation and evaluation for NPPs.
- Covers all stages from site survey through operation.
- Note: Superseded by SSG-93 but still widely referenced.

### Section 2: Site Investigation (pp. 3–17, §2.1–2.47)
- **Investigation programme (§2.1–2.24):** Progressive stages of investigation from desk study to detailed field work. Borehole depths, spacing, geophysical methods.
- **Sources of data (§2.25–2.34):** Geological maps, aerial photos, satellite imagery, existing borehole records, geological survey reports.
- **Complex subsurface conditions (§2.35–2.47):** Karst, old mine workings, expansive soils, collapsible soils, liquefiable soils, organic soils, cemented soils.

### Section 3: Site Considerations (pp. 18–25)
- **Site categorization (§3.1–3.2):** Rock sites vs. soil sites.
- **Subsurface profile parameters (§3.3–3.5):** Shear wave velocity, SPT values, soil classification.
- **Slope stability (§3.6–3.15):** Static and seismic slope stability analysis.
- **Soil liquefaction (§3.16–3.28):** Liquefaction susceptibility assessment, factor of safety, remediation options.
- **Subsidence and collapse (§3.29–3.35):** Mining subsidence, karst collapse, compaction.
- **Groundwater (§3.36–3.42):** Groundwater regime characterization, dewatering effects.

### Section 4: Foundation Evaluation (pp. 25–35)
- Bearing capacity, settlement analysis, foundation-structure interaction.
- Seismic soil-structure interaction (SSI) considerations.
- Foundation treatment options.

### Section 5: Monitoring (pp. 35–38)
- Geotechnical monitoring during construction and operation.

### Section 6: Non-NPP Installations
- Graded approach.

### Appendix: Investigation Methods
- Standard Penetration Test (SPT), Cone Penetration Test (CPT), geophysical methods, laboratory testing.

## Key Siting Criteria Extracted
- **Liquefaction (per SSG-35 Table I-1):** Massive liquefaction = exclusionary; standard liquefaction = discretionary/ranking.
- **Slope instability:** Massive landslide = exclusionary; minor instability = discretionary/ranking.
- **Subsidence:** Discretionary criterion; depends on magnitude and engineering remedy.
- **Karst (massive):** Exclusionary criterion.
- **Foundation conditions:** Ranking criterion — better foundation conditions score higher.
