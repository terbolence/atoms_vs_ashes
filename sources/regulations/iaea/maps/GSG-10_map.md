# Document Map: IAEA GSG-10
**Title:** Prospective Radiological Environmental Impact Assessment for Facilities and Activities  
**Series:** IAEA Safety Standards Series No. GSG-10 (General Safety Guide)  
**Year:** 2018 | **Pages:** 104 | **Joint sponsor:** UNEP

---

## Purpose
Provides framework and methodology for prospective radiological environmental impact assessment. Addresses both normal operation exposures and potential accident exposures. Used to assess site suitability regarding radiological impact on public and environment.

---

## Section Map

### Section 1: Introduction (pp. 1–8, §1.1–1.25)
- Guidance on meeting GSR Part 3 requirement for prospective radiological environmental impact assessment.
- Covers planned exposure situations as part of authorization process.
- Applicable to all facilities and activities involving ionizing radiation.
- Related to GSG-8 (public protection framework) and GSG-9 (discharge control).

### Section 2: Concepts and Terms (pp. 9–12, §2.1–2.14)
- Planned exposure situations: normal operation + potential exposures.
- Environmental impact assessment (EIA) framework.
- Radiological EIA as component of broader EIA.
- Members of the public definition.

### Section 3: Safety Requirements (pp. 12–16, §3.1–3.19)
- Dose limitation and dose constraints.
- Assessment for public and environmental protection.
- Potential exposure assessment and control.
- Graded approach: complexity proportional to risk.
- Transboundary impact considerations.

### Section 4: Framework for Assessment (pp. 17–25, §4.1–4.27)
- **Authorization process assessment (§4.4–4.16):**
  - Source term characterization (radionuclide inventory, release rates)
  - Environmental transfer modelling (atmospheric, aquatic pathways)
  - Exposure pathway analysis
  - Dose assessment for representative person
  - Comparison with dose constraints and limits
- **Governmental decision making (§4.17–4.21):** EIA within national regulatory framework.
- **Communication of results (§4.23–4.27):** Transparency, public information.

### Section 5: Methodology (pp. 26–51, §5.1–5.81)
- **Normal operation — public protection (§5.7–5.42):**
  - Source term estimation
  - Atmospheric dispersion modelling (Gaussian models, site-specific parameters)
  - Surface water and groundwater dispersion
  - Food chain transfer
  - Dose calculation for representative person
  - Occupancy factors, consumption rates
- **Potential exposures (§5.43–5.75):**
  - Accident scenario identification
  - Source term under accident conditions
  - Probabilistic risk assessment inputs
  - Consequence analysis
  - Risk criteria comparison
- **Environmental protection (§5.76–5.81):**
  - Reference organisms approach (ICRP)
  - Derived consideration reference levels

### Section 6: Variability and Uncertainty (pp. 51–54, §6.1–6.9)
- Parameter uncertainty, model uncertainty, scenario uncertainty.
- Sensitivity analysis recommendations.

### Appendix: Risk Criteria for Potential Exposure (pp. 55–58)
- Individual risk criteria, societal risk criteria.

### Annex I: Flora and Fauna Assessment (pp. 65–75)
- Generic methodology for non-human biota dose assessment.

## Key Relevance to Siting Project
- Provides the framework for evaluating SSG-35 Safety Set 3 (radiological impact criteria).
- Atmospheric and aquatic dispersion assessment methodology feeds into site ranking.
- Population dose assessment methodology for comparing candidate sites.
- Supports SSG-35 §4.5 criteria on dispersion characteristics.
