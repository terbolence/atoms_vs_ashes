## Purpose
Provides a comprehensive methodology and framework for developing a detailed, specific process for new nuclear power plant site selection that can withstand regulatory review. Updated for advanced nuclear technologies including SMRs. Used by utilities and developers seeking NRC (or equivalent) approval.

---

## Four-Step Methodology

### Step 1: Exclusionary Screening
**Objective:** Eliminate sites that fundamentally cannot meet regulatory or technical requirements.

**Criteria categories:**
- **Geologic/Seismic:** Capable faults, extreme seismic zones, surface deformation
- **Flooding:** 100-year/500-year/PMF floodplains without feasible protection
- **Protected Areas:** National parks, wildlife refuges, wilderness areas, tribal lands
- **Population:** Extremely high population density within emergency planning zones
- **Regulatory:** Sites within exclusion areas defined by federal/state regulations

**Process:** Binary pass/fail. Any exclusionary criterion failure eliminates the site.

### Step 2: Avoidance Screening
**Objective:** Further reduce potential sites by identifying areas with significant constraints.

**Criteria categories:**
- **Environmental:** Wetlands, endangered species habitat, cultural resources, scenic rivers
- **Land Use:** Incompatible zoning, agricultural land preservation, urban encroachment
- **Water Resources:** Insufficient cooling water, competing water demands, water quality constraints
- **Transportation:** Inadequate road/rail access for heavy equipment delivery
- **Transmission:** Distance to adequate transmission capacity
- **Social:** Community opposition indicators, environmental justice considerations

**Process:** Adjustable thresholds. Sites with multiple avoidance factors may be retained if mitigation is feasible.

### Step 3: Suitability Evaluation
**Objective:** Detailed assessment of remaining candidate sites against comprehensive criteria.

**Criteria organized in major categories:**
- **Health and Safety:**
  - Seismic and geological characteristics
  - Flooding potential and water supply reliability
  - Meteorological hazards (extreme weather, atmospheric dispersion)
  - Population density and distribution
  - Proximity to hazardous facilities and transportation routes
  - Emergency planning feasibility
- **Environmental:**
  - Aquatic ecology and thermal discharge impacts
  - Terrestrial ecology and habitat disruption
  - Air quality and radiological impact
  - Water use and consumption
  - Noise and visual impacts
- **Engineering:**
  - Foundation conditions and soil characteristics
  - Cooling water system design (once-through, cooling tower, dry cooling, hybrid)
  - Grid interconnection capacity and reliability
  - Site access for construction and operations
  - Constructability (grading, drainage, laydown areas)
- **Socioeconomic:**
  - Local economic impact (employment, tax base)
  - Community services capacity (housing, schools, healthcare)
  - Land use compatibility
  - Public acceptance indicators
  - Environmental justice considerations

**Process:** Scored evaluation using weighted criteria. Generates numerical rankings.

### Step 4: Alternative Sites Ranking
**Objective:** Final comparative ranking of highest-performing candidate sites.

**Process:**
- Cross-comparison of all scored criteria
- Sensitivity analysis on weighting assumptions
- Cost-benefit considerations
- Risk-informed decision making
- Documentation sufficient for regulatory review

## Advanced Nuclear Technology Considerations (2022 Update)

The 2022 revision specifically addresses:
- **Reduced EPZ:** SMR designs with smaller emergency planning zones expand siting options
- **Smaller Footprint:** Advanced reactors require less land, enabling more constrained sites
- **Flexible Cooling:** Some advanced designs use air cooling or reduced water needs
- **Transport Logistics:** Modular construction changes transport requirements
- **Coal-to-Nuclear:** Specific guidance on evaluating retiring coal plant sites for nuclear conversion
- **Grid Integration:** Consideration of SMR grid services (load following, hydrogen)
